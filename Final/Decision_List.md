# The decision list: what the framework changes

**Status:** draft, 5 Nov 2026, compiled from the five research briefs in `Final/research/` and the Round One plan
(`document/manuscript.md`). For Pete (and the owner of each area). It is the source for slides 3 and 6–10
(`Final_Plan.md` §4.5; slide numbers are storyline v4's, updated 12 Nov) and the list of price tags the model runs (`Final/model_runs.md`).

**The baseline is the label approach** (`Final_Plan.md` §2): what a careful planner does by default, which is what our
Round One did, so its advice fills the "before" column. On slides, say "the label approach", never "October".

**How to read it.** Each decision shows the label approach ("before"), the framework's advice, its corner (`Final_Plan.md` §4.2),
its two-sided price tag (money side, world side), the capital and who pays, when, and the evidence status. Money at
the family level (the odds the money lasts, the inheritance) comes from the model and is filled in from
`Final/model_runs.md`; everything else comes from the briefs, with their tags (SOURCED, ESTIMATED, ILLUSTRATIVE). The
business is our illustrative HK$35M-revenue distributor, Carmen owning 50% (decided, Pete, 9 Nov).

**Corners** (`Final_Plan.md` §4.2; slide 3): DO (good for their money, real effect shown) · FINANCE (good for their
money, no real effect shown: do it, don't call it green) · PAY (costs money, real effect shown: pay knowingly, from the
values budget) · REFUSE (costs money or credibility, no real effect shown: don't pay for the label). In REFUSE, a
person's values may still buy a product from the values budget, named as alignment, never as impact ("We don't refuse
Carmen's values. We refuse the label."). Aligned 13 Nov: PAY used to read "real effect shown or reflects their values",
which put Carmen's ESG funds in two corners at once.

## 3. Each decision's price tag

Money at the family level (odds, inheritance) is in `Final/model_runs.md`; the lines below give what the briefs
established and point to the run.

### B1. Electric vans, LED, office air-con (`area2_business.md` §3c–3d)
- **Money (business):** saves ≈HK$303K a year before tax with diesel at **HK$31/L, the government's average after
  forecourt discounts** (Aug 2026, HK$30.91–31.51; LCQ11 Annex I, 7 Oct 2026); HK$372K at the HK$37.93/L pump list,
  HK$243K at HK$25/L. Payback ≈6.3 years on full capex (8-year van life). **The saving no longer covers a 5-year
  loan** (≈HK$349K a year): the company tops up ≈HK$46K a year to 2032, or borrows over longer (bank terms
  UNVERIFIED). Debt cover 5.7× before, ≈3× after (at list diesel 3.3×). The
  electric van costs ≈HK$12.4K more than diesel after the first-registration-tax waiver, ≈HK$83K more if the waiver
  lapses after 31 Mar 2028.
- **Money (family):** through the sale price (extra profit × the sale multiple × Carmen's share × the plan's 60% haircut)
  and, as a variant, through dividends. Run M2.
- **Cost per tonne (slide 7's cost curve):** the vans ≈−HK$12,800 a tonne at HK$31/L (13 Nov: area2_business.md §3c's
  own method, the premium plus a charger annualised at 6% over 8 years, rerun at HK$31/L; it gives −16,400 at the pump
  list and −9,700 at HK$25/L, as the brief does). LED ≈−HK$2,000, air-con ≈−HK$1,200 (the brief). ESTIMATED.
- **World:** 26.1 t a year (vans 19.3, LED 4.1, air-con 2.8), 54% of Scope 1+2, against
  replacing them with new diesel vans (the counterfactual we state; owning the project doesn't by itself prove the
  cut additional). Fuel and kWh are measured once running; the tonnes are estimated from them with published factors.
- **Green finance:** a green label on the loan only if free (self-certified or the bank's SME framework); net greenium
  ≈HK$0 (B2). **CHECK:** a fleet diesel price.

### B2. Refuse a sustainability-linked loan and paid green labels at this size (`area2_business.md` §3d; `area2_supply_chain.md` §3c)
- **Money:** a ratchet on HK$0.9M is worth HK$450–4,500 a year against ≈HK$12K a year of verification; it breaks even only
  above HK$4.8M of debt at 25bp. A 25bp green discount on HK$1.5M saves ≈HK$12K over five years, the cost of the
  cheapest assessment. The HKMA grant needs at least HK$100M (`hook_fact_checks.md`). Bank supply-chain finance: anchors
  at ≈US$100M of trade spend; her purchases are ≈US$3.4M.
- **World:** none: the vans happen either way.

### B3. Emissions data (`area2_business.md` §3e; `area2_supply_chain.md` §3c)
- **Money (business):** costs ≈HK$54–73K a year. Protects ≈HK$209K of Carmen's stake per 1% of sales kept; pays for
  itself at 0.6% of sales. Her listed customers report Scope 3 from FY2026 (LargeCap; `hook_fact_checks.md`).
- **Money (family):** a sensitivity, not a forecast: if a customer worth 5% or 10% of sales leaves for a supplier with
  better data, the sale proceeds fall. Run M3.
- **World:** no tonnes by itself; it is what makes B4 and supplier choices measurable.
- **Evidence:** no study measures an SME sale premium for emissions data; we model value through revenue kept, not a
  multiple premium.

### B4. Recycled content (`area2_supply_chain.md` §4)
- **Money (business):** ≈HK$0 at resin parity: food-grade rPET pellets and virgin bottle-grade PET both US$1,240/t FOB
  Southeast Asia on 7 Oct 2026 (Platts, via S&P's headline and a vendor blog: medium confidence), because the Middle
  East war pushed virgin prices up. At the 2025 Asian peak (US$485/t premium, mid-Aug 2025; S&P *Chemical Trends H1
  2026*, p.27, a PDF a judge can open) it would be ≈HK$248K a year on 65.5 t, ≈2.3 HK cents a cup (ESTIMATED), passed
  to customers as an itemised line. Parity is a window, not a fact for good: resin isn't packaging (conversion and
  certified food-contact supply add cost, UNVERIFIED). Not the family's values budget.
- **Cost per tonne (slide 7):** ≈HK$0 at parity; ≈+HK$2,300 a tonne at the 2025 peak premium (HK$248K ÷ 108 t,
  13 Nov). ESTIMATED.
- **World:** ≈108 t a year (Scope 3, purchased goods; ESTIMATED with DESNZ factors).
- **Caveat:** Phase 2 of the plastics rules would ban plastic tableware regardless; aim recycled content at
  pre-packaged and retail packaging, fibre at tableware.

### B5. Stop "compostable" claims (`area2_supply_chain.md` §2b; `area2_business.md`)
- **Money:** protects the tableware stock exposed to the plastics rules (≈HK$0.77M in the business brief: CHECK the

- **World:** no tonnes; it stops a label that misleads. The greenwashing control on Carmen's own business.

### B6. Green lease at the 2027 renewal (`area4_property_scenario.md` §4)

- **World:** small. Clauses: reinstatement waiver, shared data, cost recovery capped at measured savings, a renewal

### B7. The sale (`area2_business.md` §3e)

  30% his tracking error was ≈0.63%, above his limit. Fees unchanged, as EDMW and IWDA both charge 0.20%. Earlier: 27.7%,
