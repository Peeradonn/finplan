Eligibility 
This is a team competition. Each team consists of at least 3 or at most 4 students. Each student can only participate in one team and all members of a team must be from the same university. All full time undergraduate students are eligible :

Competition Rules
Formation of team name: university names and related words to be incorporated into team name
is prohibited. Please
do NOT mention your university names and related words in your financial proposal submission and during your final presentation. Panel of judges will not know teams from which universities until Final Round results are obtained.
There are Three (3) Rounds of competitions.
It is a requirement that each team MUST send at least ONE team member to attend the financial planning workshop organized by Society of Registered Financial Planners (HKRFP). Teams fail to comply will be disqualified. 
Round One (12 top score teams selected, out of which 8 top score teams enter into Round Two)
Written financial proposal submitted to HKRFP in English.
HKRFP will provide a case to be uploaded on HKRFP web site after workshop.
Each participating team should prepare a detailed financial proposal from the given case.
The proposal MUST be written in English and no more than 15 single-sided, single-spaced pages, with 12-point Times Roman font size, one space need (including cover pages and appendices)
Criterion Effective use of client’s information (15 scores) Make use of all the information provided in the case to assess the current financial conditions of the client.
Formulation of financial goals (10 scores) Help the client establish realistic financial goals given his/her constraints.
Personal statement (15 scores) Discussion of personal career goal and opportunities under the cross-boundary wealth management connect scheme.
Financial proposal (25 scores) Devise and defend a comprehensive financial proposal that is most suitable for the client to achieve his/her financial goals.
Risk Analysis (15 scores) Identify risks that may arise in the financial proposal and suggest safeguards to mitigate these risks.
Presentation (20 scores) Clarity, organization, conciseness, grammar.
Round Two (8 teams from Round One, out of which 4 top score teams selected to enter Final Round)
Each winners team is required to submit the following to HKRFP by e-mail info@hkrfp.org. A 10 minutes video oral presentation of their written case in Round One in English, and
A set of Power point slides of the presentation.
Criterion Communication skills (45 scores) Present ideas clearly, systematically, effectively and forcefully.
Content delivery  - Personal statement (15 scores) Presentation of personal statement and coverage of the cross boundary wealth management connect scheme.
Content delivery - Financial proposal (20 scores) Presentation of essential elements of a financial proposal within the time constrain provided.
Power-point slides (ppt) (15 scores) Quality of ppt.
Others (5 scores) Charisma and team spirit.
Teams are ranked according to: combined scores of 0.5  x Round One scores + 0.5 x Round Two scores.
Final Round – Presentation on topical issue A topical issue related to financial planning will be provided by HKRFP.
Each team has 10 minutes to present in front of a panel of judges in English.
Presentation will be followed by 10 minutes questions and answers session from panel of judges.
Each team is required  to send the the power-point slides of their presentation before Final Round and Award Presentation Ceremony by e-mail to info@hkrfp.org
Criterion Knowledge of the topic (20 scores)
Logical thinking (20 scores)
Presentation skills (20 scores)
Innovations, independent thinking (40 scores)
Teams are ranked according to: combined scores of 0.15 x Round One scores + 0.15 x
Round Two scores + 0.7 x Final scores.
