## How can the family distinguish investment results from sustainability claims?

### Takeaway

**High confidence:** use an investable conventional twin agreed before measurement, with the same financial job and the same cash-flow calendar. Its return is the baseline, not an attribution component of the difference. Report the remaining difference as an observed package difference; it is not automatically a causal ESG effect. Net fund returns already incorporate the costs reflected in those returns, so adding the headline fee again can double count it.

### Cited findings

- The competition explicitly asks for financial, sustainability, impact and implementation outcomes, attribution distinct from narrative, five named biases, decision rights, review triggers and a funded legacy allocation. These are broader requirements than a carbon dashboard. Primary local sources read in full: [Final topic](../../Final/2026%20UFP%20Final%20Round%20Topic.pdf), pp.3–4; [Key areas](../../Final/2026%20UFP%20Final%20Round%20Topic%20Key%20Areas.pdf), p.2 (2026; accessed 8 Nov 2026). These relative links are source paths for the writer; final brief should link from its own location correctly.
- The actual case distinguishes Adrian's low-to-medium and Carmen's medium-to-high risk profiles, Ryan's independent finances, retirement at 65/62, education flexibility, and a possible educational/environmental philanthropy plan. ESG alignment of at least 20–25% is requested; no fee, tracking-error, donation or review threshold is agreed. [Case](../../Primary%20Info/Award2026Case20260919.pdf), pp.2,5–7 (19 Sep 2026).
- GIPS distinguishes time-weighted measurement, which seeks to neutralise external cash-flow effects, from money-weighted experience. It requires consistent cash-flow handling and geometric linking of subperiod returns. Its gross-of-fees returns already deduct transaction costs; net-of-fees returns additionally deduct investment-management fees, and other deductions require disclosure. Household use of these principles does **not** establish GIPS compliance. Source: [GIPS Standards Handbook for Firms](https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/), 2020 standards, effective 1 Jan 2020; sections 2.A.13, 2.A.31 and 4.C.7; current handbook read 8 Nov 2026. **High confidence.**

### Inferences

#### A workable conventional twin (proposal, not an external standard)

1. Record benchmark mapping at inception, with version, date and reason. Use purchasable, suitable instruments and actual channel fees, not a free index as the only economic alternative.
2. Match strategic equity/bond/cash weights, issuer-credit quality, duration, currency exposure and hedging, geography, distribution/reinvestment, liquidity and tax/estate constraints. Match initial capital, contribution/withdrawal dates and rebalancing rule. Use each parent's own sleeve, plus an aggregated household view. Ryan's money is not available to fund parental commitments without his separate decision.
3. Climate equity index versus its parent can isolate a specified *index-construction* difference more cleanly than a generic ESG fund versus the world. A green-bond fund versus a global aggregate fund can have sovereign/corporate, duration, credit, currency and country differences; those must be disclosed. The cheapest global equity MPF fund may not match the low-carbon fund's geography, hedge or index. Do not call an imperfect match an otherwise-identical twin.
4. Measure net total returns in HKD on matching dates. For a USD return with no hedge, exact conversion is `(1+r_USD) × (HKD per USD at end / HKD per USD at start) − 1`. Use actual FX records. A USD trading line does not establish its underlying economic currency exposure. Report no invented conversion rate.
5. Keep two outputs: time-weighted return for strategy comparison; actual cash flows, terminal wealth and money-weighted return for the family's experience. Show actual/simulated status, nominal or real basis, horizon, dates and fee convention. Do not subtract two annualised returns and multiply by today's capital as though it were the actual historic annual cash loss.

#### Exact reconciliation before explanations (original algebra)

For one period, matched weights/currencies/cash-flow conventions, let `r_EI` be the sustainable index return, `r_PI` its conventional parent, `r_EF` the sustainable fund's NAV total return, and `r_PF` the conventional fund's NAV total return. Define observed wrapper gaps `d_E=r_EF−r_EI` and `d_P=r_PF−r_PI`. Then exactly:

`r_EF − r_PF = (r_EI − r_PI) + d_E − d_P`.

The first term is index-design difference, not proof of ESG alpha. The wrapper gaps can already contain fund fees, tax, replication, securities lending and cash effects. Explain those components only where accounts support them; otherwise retain an honest unexplained residual. Do not add TER/OCF on top of a net NAV gap. External dealing spread, commission, custody, platform/adviser charges and actual withholding effects belong only where not already included. Avoid double counting a tax adjustment when comparing a net-tax index to fund returns.

For a whole policy, create nested portfolios under identical cash flows: `T` conventional policy, `S` sustainable policy with the same allocation/rebalance discipline, `A` actual implementation. `r_A−r_T=(r_S−r_T)+(r_A−r_S)` is a telescoping reconciliation. The sustainable package term includes relevant embedded costs. The actual-policy term may combine timing, allocation deviations and implementation costs; separating it needs trade and valuation data. Across periods link returns geometrically; a sum of annual active returns is not cumulative wealth attribution. For family cash-flow comparisons reconcile ending wealth in HKD with the common external cash-flow schedule.

**Worked example, entirely illustrative; not Wong data or a product forecast.** One nominal year, HK$1,000,000 starting capital in each alternative, all dividends reinvested, no external cash flow, and external charges deducted at year end solely to make the arithmetic transparent. Conventional index +5.0%; sustainable index +4.5%; conventional fund NAV total return +4.8%; sustainable fund +4.2%. Fund fee effects are inside NAV returns. External charges HK$2,000 conventional and HK$4,000 sustainable. Conventional ending wealth HK$1,046,000; sustainable HK$1,038,000; difference **−HK$8,000**. Reconciliation: index design −HK$5,000; differential wrapper effect −HK$1,000 (`−3,000−(−2,000)`); differential external costs −HK$2,000. Sum −HK$8,000. Adding a separate fund-management fee to this result would count it twice if already in the NAV return. Positive market performance does not make the relative sacrifice disappear; the relative sacrifice does not mean the fund lost money outright.

**Optional sector attribution, only with data.** For a single period, exhaustive mutually exclusive sectors, beginning weights and consistent total returns, the Brinson–Fachler allocation `Σ(wP−wB)(rB_sector−rB_total)` plus selection `ΣwB(rP_sector−rB_sector)` must include interaction `Σ(wP−wB)(rP_sector−rB_sector)` to reconcile active return. This is an algebraic identity under those assumptions. Alternatively combine selection and interaction using portfolio sector weights and explicitly name the convention. The old framework gives allocation and selection only, omitting interaction. Public end-date sector weights and a headline return are insufficient; transaction effects, changing weights and multiperiod linking require more. A factor regression can describe exposures but does not identify an ESG treatment effect. **Recommendation:** omit sector/factor claims from the first client dashboard unless the data actually reconcile.

## Which behavioural safeguards are defensible for this family?

### Takeaway

The five biases are risks in *our advice process*, not diagnoses of the Wongs. Use neutral, private trade-offs, consistent uncertainty displays, dated implementation and a household-wide cost view. No cited experiment proves these safeguards will improve this family's retirement result.

### Cited findings

- **Present bias/inertia:** Thaler and Benartzi describe advance commitment of future pay increases, automatic escalation and opt-out rights. Their three workplace implementations differ; they explicitly say design components were not individually isolated and implementation choices reflected employers' preferences. Evidence for practical commitment is stronger than an assertion that the specific mechanism is proven for a philanthropic household pledge. [Save More Tomorrow](https://ab-d.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/thaler2004.pdf), *Journal of Political Economy* 112(1, part 2), Feb 2004, pp.S164–S187; pp.S170–S171 read. **High confidence on reported design; moderate external validity.**
- **Ambiguity aversion:** Ellsberg's original discussion distinguishes uncertainty in probabilities from known risk; conflicting/relevance/reliability information can make estimates ambiguous. It explicitly resists declaring ambiguity-sensitive choices foolish. [Risk, Ambiguity, and the Savage Axioms](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~bskyrms/bio/readings/ellsberg.pdf), *QJE* 75(4), Nov 1961, pp.643–669; pp.659–669 read. **High confidence concept; not a household clinical test.**
- **Social desirability/image:** Ariely, Bracha and Meier randomise visibility in a charitable-effort experiment; public visibility alters prosocial effort and its interaction with private incentives. Their field extension has acknowledged selection limits. This supports attention to the interview setting, not a presumption that Carmen is insincere. [Doing Good or Doing Well?](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3130/AER_March2009_v99n1p544.pdf), *AER* 99(1), March 2009, pp.544–555, especially experiment and pp.552–553. **High confidence experiment; limited transfer.**
- **Genuine preferences are a strong counterweight:** Riedl and Smeets connect administrative SRI holdings with survey/incentivised-experiment data and report both social preferences and signalling. Source accessed was the publisher abstract, **not full article**. [Why Do Investors Hold Socially Responsible Mutual Funds?](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.12547), first published 1 Aug 2017. Bauer, Ruof and Smeets' real pension-policy voting study finds support among low as well as high signalling groups, so signalling does not explain all support. [Get Real!](https://www.fir-pri-awards.org/wp-content/uploads/Article_Bauer-Ruof-Smeets.pdf), *RFS* 34(8), 2021, pp.3976–4043, especially pp.4009–4010. **High confidence distinction; not representative of Hong Kong households.**
- **Loss aversion/framing:** Kahneman and Tversky's prospect theory describes reference-dependent values, losses weighted more strongly than comparable gains and framing effects in risky choices; it is not a calibrated risk profile for Adrian. [Prospect Theory](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf), *Econometrica* 47(2), March 1979, pp.263–292, especially pp.278–280. **High confidence concept; no universal numerical loss-aversion multiplier assumed.**
- **Mental accounting:** Thaler describes budget categories, treatment of windfalls and evaluation frequency; these can break fungibility. Accounts may also serve self-control. [Mental Accounting Matters](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/thaler-jbdm1999.pdf), *JBDM* 12(3), Sept 1999, pp.183–206. **High confidence mechanism; not evidence that all earmarking is harmful.**

A substantive annual family review with intervening event monitoring is a proportionate **proposal**, not a behavioural theorem or a reason to hide monthly information. ### Gaps

- No family elicitation has occurred. Fee tolerance, drawdown capacity, liquidity sacrifice, philanthropic priorities and role acceptance all need direct answers. Case descriptions cannot supply numerical budgets.
- Original behavioural papers identify mechanisms in other settings. Whether this process reduces delays, improves comprehension and prevents unsuitable decisions should be checked through this family's action log and teach-back, not assumed.
- What would change the design: evidence that private-first questions confuse or distress the family, or that the proposed monitoring is too burdensome. Simplify the process while retaining informed consent and urgent alerts.

## What governance and dashboard should turn evidence into action?

### Takeaway

Every displayed result needs a decision it can change, an evidence owner and an escalation path. The annex should distinguish the individual owner's values from joint financial constraints, and shareholder engagement from a claim of caused impact. Roles and limits below are proposals requiring the relevant client's consent and the business's actual authority.

### Cited findings

- SFC suitability guidance expects client knowledge, product due diligence, suitable recommendations, information and retained reasons; it is a dynamic assessment of each client's circumstances, not mechanical matching of risk scores. Incomplete/out-of-date information needs attention, and explanations should be balanced with time to consider. [Compliance with Suitability Obligations](https://www.sfc.hk/en/faqs/intermediaries/supervision/Compliance-with-Suitability-Obligations/Compliance-with-Suitability-Obligations), current page last updated **23 Jul 2026**, Q1–Q2, Q5–Q6. This concerns licensed/registered persons' obligations; it does not license the student team to sell products. **High confidence.**
- For portfolio mandates/model portfolios, SFC discusses regular review, e.g. annual or significant market movements, using latest client circumstances, agreed revisions and a written rationale. This is mandate-specific guidance, not a statutory annual household sustainability-report requirement. [Triggering of Suitability Obligations](https://www.sfc.hk/en/faqs/intermediaries/supervision/Triggering-of-Suitability-Obligations/Triggering-of-Suitability-Obligations), last updated **27 Dec 2018**, Q3. **High confidence.**
- SFC responsible-ownership principles are voluntary and non-binding; external engagement services do not eliminate the investor's responsibility for consistency with its own policy. [Principles of Responsible Ownership](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Rules-and-standards/Principles-of-responsible-ownership), last updated **7 Mar 2016**. A household can borrow the discipline without claiming a regulatory obligation to maintain an institutional stewardship team. **High confidence.**

### Inferences

#### Proposed sustainability annex and decision record

The annex should record: (1) assets and account owners in scope, distinguishing household, business and Ryan's assets; (2) liquidity/retirement/education constraints and ordinary risk mandate; (3) each person's values exclusions and preferences, neutral/unsure allowed; (4) what counts as alignment versus impact and what evidence is acceptable; (5) explicit cost/relative-risk budgets chosen after HK$ scenario discussions, rather than inherited illustrative 5bp/3%/80% limits; (6) the twin mapping and calculation conventions; (7) data dictionary and restatement policy; (8) who may decide, who checks, and what requires joint consent; (9) review and engagement escalation; (10) funding source and terms of giving. Version, date and relevant signatures/consents matter. A dashboard cannot substitute for the mandate.

Suggested decision record fields: ID; date; asset/account owner and legal decision-maker; problem; options including defer/do-nothing where feasible; financial and sustainability evidence with dates/quality; estimated cost and uncertainty; client trade-off/consent; dissent and resolution; approved action, owner and deadline; completion evidence; next review trigger; links to metric IDs and any restatements. Changed benchmark or rule must retain the old version and reason; do not improve history by silently changing the benchmark. A correction can legitimately alter comparability; keep original and recalculated values visible.

Joint assets use their documented joint authority and agreed protected goals; neither parent's preferences automatically override the other. If unresolved, defer new discretionary values spending while maintaining suitable existing authorised arrangements and necessary obligations. Automatically moving everything to a plain twin could itself be an unauthorised, costly change.
- Carmen leads business information requests only within her actual management authority. The assumed 50% share does not establish unilateral capital-expenditure/dividend/lease or financing authority; verify shareholder agreement, board/signatory rules and lender covenants. Company savings, debt service and family dividends remain separate.
- A licensed adviser can maintain and check the dashboard. An appointed family data contact can upload statements; Ryan is a candidate only if he agrees, has time and receives consent to access personal data. Data entry is distinct from verification; an experienced analyst is not automatically a carbon assurer. Chloe can be consulted appropriately; do not describe her as having signed an adult investment mandate.
- The family proposes an annual joint decision meeting, lighter intervening checks proportionate to materiality, and event-driven escalation. Actual loan covenants, insurance renewal dates, fund notice periods and grant deadlines take precedence over a generic annual rhythm.
- If a new liquidity/health/education need threatens a protected objective, refresh the financial plan before authorising new values spending or irreversible gifts. Once a gift is irrevocably transferred, future market losses cannot be solved by assuming the charity will return it.

#### Engagement is a workflow, not a meeting count

For a fund, request the relevant issuer-level voting and engagement record, objective, milestone, deadline, response and evidence of the manager's contribution. Distinguish the manager's actions from the household's fund ownership. If evidence is weak: ask for clarification, set a proportionate response date, compare an alternative, discuss retaining with a weaker claim or replacing within financial constraints, and record the choice. A vote or meeting is an activity, a changed corporate policy an intermediate outcome, implemented emissions reduction a further result; none alone proves the family's investment caused it. For Carmen's suppliers/landlord, record an achievable requested change, consent/data rights, delivery milestones and operational verification. Group engagement and negotiations can matter, but do not count contacts as tonnes reduced.

Event triggers should be grounded in obligations: unavailable product, changed index/mandate/fees, credible material controversy, failed reporting condition, coverage/methodology deterioration that invalidates the comparison, changed client needs, loan covenant, lease renewal, insurer exclusions, verified grant or regulatory deadline. A method breach may require immediate correction of a public claim even when selling the asset would be premature. A tracking-error threshold, once agreed, triggers reassessment; it does not automatically authorise trading or insure against a future loss.
