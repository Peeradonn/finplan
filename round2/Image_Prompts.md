> shading, warm natural skin tones, friendly and calm. Colour palette limited to near-black #121212, one crimson
> #A81E2D used sparingly, light greys #F2F2F2, #D9D9D9 and #A0A0A0, white, and warm light wood. Plain white background.
> No text, letters, numbers, signs, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.
