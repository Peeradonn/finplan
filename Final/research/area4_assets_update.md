# Make property decisions earn their place

**Competition Area 4: Real Assets, Property and Climate Resilience**

**Verdict — 8 November 2026.** Area 4 should recommend conditional investment and condition-based housing reviews. The evidence does **not** support a blanket refusal of green refinancing, a compulsory sale in 2045, or describing reverse mortgage as a complete climate hedge. A useful retrofit must clear its service-equivalent replacement cost, operating uncertainty and enforceable ownership/lease horizon; a useful financing offer must improve the all-in outcome after fees. Physical-risk evidence supports site inspection, policy review and stress liquidity, but cannot establish a Wong-specific climate loss probability or property discount. The strongest demonstrable improvement is therefore a decision process with explicit thresholds: inspect and measure, price the alternatives, choose knowingly, and monitor the result. No new family-specific retirement success probability is established by this brief.

Evidence cutoff and access date: **8 November 2026**. Lenses used: practitioner judge, family, insurance/compliance, ESG strategist and landlord–tenant implementation. **High confidence** means explicit primary terms or arithmetic; **medium confidence** means interpretation/version uncertainty; **unverified** identifies missing family facts or unread terms. All worked financial examples are **illustrative research calculations**, not quotations, measured savings or presentation-approved model outputs. Existing briefs, plans, models and frozen submissions remain unchanged.

## Establish the asset and the decision rights first

Area 4 asks how the family's home and business premises should change financial planning: financing and certification, equipment and building works, physical hazards, ageing/exit risk, and the landlord–tenant split incentive. In plain English, the question is whether an asset still provides the housing or operating service the family needs, at an affordable total cost, and who can authorise and benefit from improvements. A green mortgage or certificate is one input; it does not establish suitability, savings or resilience. The competition's primary materials require assumptions and measurable change, rather than a predetermined product recommendation. ## Finance and certification must clear their full cost

### Compare eligible offers, including an ordinary alternative

Public green terms are materially different. **BEA** includes refinancing for qualifying BEAM Plus Gold/Platinum New/Existing Buildings, minimum loan HK$1M; its extra green rebate starts at HK$2M, so the published table gives **zero green rebate at HK$1.8M**. The application promotion ends 31 December 2026. **BOCHK's April–December 2026 promotion excludes refinancing**, while **HSBC's live green page concerns first-hand purchases on its designated building list**; HSBC's current incentive amount is not stated there. Its detailed 2026 PDF was not readable in this research pass, so this brief does not independently endorse every clause quoted in the old notes. ([BEA terms](https://www.hkbea.com/html/en/Green_Mortgage.html), [BOCHK terms](https://www.bochk.com/dam/mortgage/green/tandc_e.pdf), [HSBC product](https://www.hsbc.com.hk/mortgages/products/home-loans/green/))

**Hang Seng's standalone factsheet** offers an additional HK$1,000, minimum loan HK$1M, qualifying building Gold/Platinum, with drawdown by **31 December 2026**. It includes transfer mortgages but excludes “re-mortgage”; confirm the bank's transaction classification. Its combined booklet still shows 30 June 2026, a material version conflict: require a dated written offer. Ordinary rebates, rates, legal costs and the outgoing bank's charges remain separate. ([Standalone factsheet](https://www.hangseng.com/content/dam/wpb/hase/rwd/personal/mortgages/green-mortgages/pdf/green_mortgage_factsheet.pdf), [combined terms](https://www.hangseng.com/content/dam/wpb/hase/config/bde/pws/personal/mortgage/pdfs/mortgage_terms.pdf))

The Consumer Council found no better green mortgage rates among its surveyed plans in **February–March 2023**. That supports scepticism, not a universal November 2026 pricing claim. Nor does a 1996 completion prove either certification or its absence. **Recommended wording:** “We would not refinance for this rebate alone. Verify eligibility and require a positive all-in saving against the best suitable ordinary offer.” The counter-case is straightforward: if the family already qualifies and would refinance anyway, an incremental reward can be useful. A written better green rate would change the verdict immediately. ([Consumer Council, 17 April 2023](https://www.consumer.org.hk/en/press-release/p-558-green-mortgage))

For identical starting debt and horizon, calculate:

`Refinance NPV = incremental rewards − switching/certification costs + PV(payment savings) + PV(old residual debt − new residual debt)`.

Include legal/valuation/redemption costs, early-repayment charges, rebate clawbacks, account/deposit requirements and opportunity cost. **Extending the tenor lowers payments without necessarily creating a saving.** Illustratively, HK$1.8M amortising over 132 months at 3.50% versus 3.40% saves HK$84.99 monthly. At a nominal 3% annual discount rate divided monthly, PV savings are HK$9,545; with HK$10,000 costs and a HK$1,000 incremental reward, NPV is only **+HK$545**; without the reward it is **−HK$455**. The ten-basis-point difference and costs are invented comparison inputs, not bank quotes. The formula is `payment=P*(a/12)/(1−(1+a/12)^−132)`; discount each month at `1+.03/12`.

### A flat-level certificate is not a whole-building rating

BEAM Plus Interiors Residential covers an individual fitted-out existing home, using Green/Green+ ratings and a five-year certificate. The fee scale effective **1 April 2026** lists HK$110 registration plus HK$500 assessment at internal floor area ≤100 m², or HK$1,000 assessment above it. These exclude works and professional/compliance costs. Bank whole-building Gold/Platinum requirements do not automatically accept this route. For the assumed warehouse, 7,500 sq ft is **696.77 m²**; if this is eligible one-floor internal assessment area, registration HK$1,210 plus assessment HK$29,000 totals **HK$30,210**. Warehouse scope, area basis and floor split need BSL confirmation. ([Residential route](https://www.hkgbc.org.hk/eng/beam-plus/beam-plus-interiors/index_2_0_r.jsp), [fee scale dated 11 February 2026](https://www.hkgbc.org.hk/eng/beam-plus/beam-plus-references/fee/FeeFiles/BI_2_0_Fee_Scale_V2-0.pdf))

HKMC's **2026 RMP green promotion** does accept a qualifying residential interior certificate, as well as specified whole-building ratings. The reward is the higher of HK$3,000 and 0.1% of specified property value, capped HK$8,000; **application and drawdown must both occur by 31 December 2026**, with continued-participation conditions. Both parents are under the usual minimum age 55 in 2026. This is no 2037 entitlement. The separate March 2026 RMP rate enhancement is **not green-conditional**. ([RMP promotion factsheet, March 2026](https://www.hkmc.com.hk/files/product_file/1/1472/Green%20(RMP)%20Factsheet_Final_2026-03%20(Eng)_Web%20Accessibility.pdf), [9 March 2026 announcement](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202603/09/P2026030900310p.htm))

**Recommendation:** price physical work separately from optional certification. Certify for a named financial, customer or personal benefit whose full cost the decision-maker accepts; do not assume a causal resale premium, verified kWh saving or flood protection.

## Retrofits succeed through utilisation, timing and tenure

### Use incremental lifecycle NPV and the actual bill

The recommended method is `NPV = −incremental installed cost + PV(avoided bills + avoided maintenance + approved grants − extra operating/verification costs) + PV(residual proceeds − reinstatement)`. Match useful illumination/cooling, horizon and service quality. At failure, compare an efficient replacement with an ordinary replacement; for early replacement, compare buying now with retaining the working asset until realistic failure. Include permits, downtime, disposal, financing and interactions between lighting/cooling/fabric. Use either project cash flows with an appropriate capital cost or equity cash flows including loan proceeds and repayments; do not double-count principal.

CLP's 2026 Non-Residential Tariff energy charge is **110.6 cents/kWh**, plus monthly fuel adjustment. The official adjustment effective **1 November 2026** is **47.4 cents**, so an NRT account above low-use rebate thresholds faces a marginal rate of **HK$1.58/kWh** for this illustration. The system average HK$1.406 is inappropriate. Residential blocks require a before/after whole-bill calculation; November pricing is no forecast. ([2026 table, effective 1 January](https://www.clp.com.hk/content/dam/clphk/documents/tariff-adjustment-2026/Tariff%20Table%20-%20English_Update.pdf), [monthly adjustment](https://www.clp.com.hk/en/help-support/bills-payment-tariffs/fuel-cost-adjustment))

The following examples use **nominal HK$, flat nominal prices, 5% nominal discounting and annual end-year cash flows**. All equipment, hours, costs and lives are hypothetical. No tax benefit, causal property premium or grant is included unless named. The independent standard-library [calculator](../../research_notes/Hong%20Kong%20property%20decision%20evidence/area4_retrofit_calcs.py) and [results](../../research_notes/Hong%20Kong%20property%20decision%20evidence/area4_retrofit_results.json) were run and independently reproduced; they do not import the retirement model.

| Warehouse LED | 100 existing 42W whole circuits →18W; same illumination; 3,000 hours/year; installed HK$22,000; maintenance saving HK$500/year; three enforceable lease years; no residual/reinstatement | `100×24/1000×3000=7,200 kWh/year`; annual benefit HK$11,876 at HK$1.58; **company NPV +HK$10,341**, simple payback 1.85 years |

| Same LED, low use | 1,200 hours/year and HK$1.20/kWh, three years | **NPV −HK$11,227**; even a conditional HK$6,000 year-one grant leaves −HK$5,513 |
| Same LED, reinstatement/high use | Base plus HK$5,000 reinstatement at year 3; alternatively 4,500 hours and HK$1.90/kWh | **+HK$6,022** with reinstatement; **+HK$35,243** high-use scenario. Base no-grant break-even is about **1,999 hours/year** |

| Home AC before failure | Buy HK$7,000 now vs in three years; 400 kWh/year saved only during first three years at HK$1.50; eight-year matched horizon; assumed remaining-service values HK$1,400 vs HK$3,500 | `−7000+7000/1.05³+600×sum(1.05^−t,t=1..3)−2100/1.05⁸` = **−HK$741**; residual values are assumptions, not resale quotes |
| Window film | HK$3,000, 100 kWh/year at HK$1.50, ten years | **−HK$1,842**; needs about 259 kWh/year. Orientation, permissions, daylight and ventilation remain unknown |

For the base LED example, Carmen's assumed 50% **economic share** is HK$5,171, not guaranteed household income. An illustrative 8% three-year annual-amortisation loan needs HK$8,537/year, giving project saving/debt-service coverage 1.39 before other company debt. Financing can make the up-front payment manageable without curing low utilisation. Do not book both the same company savings as household distributions and as a capitalised business-sale uplift.

CLP EEUS can support an eligible business account holder, including a tenant; its NRT guideline covers **1 January 2024–31 December 2028**, funding permitting. The scheme-period account cap is **HK$10,000**, not annual. Eligible LED tubes receive the lower of HK$60 each or 80% of net purchase price; apply within six months of purchase and satisfy eligibility checks. If 100 tubes genuinely qualify, unused quota exists and HK$6,000 is approved/received at year-one end, base LED NPV becomes **+HK$16,056**. The no-grant case should stand on its own. Communal installations under Eco Building Fund are a separate route; do not allocate an OC subsidy automatically to tenant equipment. ([EEUS guideline, 17 January 2024](https://www.clp.com.hk/content/dam/clphk/documents/my-business-site/low-carbon-solutions/eeus/EEUS_NRT%20Application%20Guideline_bilingual.pdf), [Eco Building Fund FAQ](https://www.clp.com.hk/en/business/low-carbon-solutions/funds-and-subsidies/eco-building-fund/faq))

### Solar receipts, environmental attributes and lease incentives differ

A non-owner can apply under CLP FiT with owner consent. The linked contract ends **31 December 2033**, transfers environmental attributes to CLP, restricts the seller/affiliates from claiming solar-powered premises or associated emissions reductions, and charges gross electricity consumption. Do **not** add avoided retail bills to the same FiT generation income or claim the sold attributes as tenant emissions reductions. The 2022 government rates are capacity tiers; the actual application/completion letter governs the rate. ([CLP terms, 10 June 2023, §§3.3,5.1–5.3](https://www.clp.com.hk/content/dam/clphk/documents/fit/Terms%20and%20Conditions%20%28Eng%29_10%20Jun%202023.pdf), [government rates, effective 27 April 2022](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202204/26/P2022042600448.htm))

Illustratively, 1kWp installed at start-2027 for HK$20,000, yielding 1,000 kWh in year one, degrading 0.5% yearly, earning HK$4/kWh and costing HK$200/year O&M has **+HK$1,666 NPV** over seven payments through 2033. With only three enforceable roof/lease years and no departure compensation it has **−HK$9,704 NPV**. No later cash or residual value is credited; later self-consumption needs separate terms. Roof rights, structure/wind loading, fire access, waterproofing, yield and prices need professional confirmation. For EV charging, EHSS **closed to new applications on 31 December 2023**; ongoing funded works do not establish a new 2026 entitlement. Check any existing estate approval and actual charging need. ([Official EHSS, revised 5 February 2025](https://www.evhomecharging.gov.hk/en))

A useful green lease specifies consent, equipment ownership/removal, metering/data access, a weather/activity-adjusted baseline, grants, savings sharing, repairs, reinstatement, landlord break/assignment compensation and dispute/true-up procedures. These are proposed commercial requirements for legal review, consistent with Hong Kong guidance, not mandatory law. A longer term has rent and flexibility costs; do not assume a 2039 renewal raises the business-sale price. ([HKGBC Green Office Guide, pp.130–132](https://www.hkgbc.org.hk/eng/resources/publications/HKGBC-Publication/Guidebook/Hong_Kong_Green_Office_Guide_English_Optimised.pdf))

Savings sharing cannot manufacture profitability. With illustrative landlord capex HK$50,000 and total tenant net savings HK$7,000/year, a 70% landlord share over five years at 5% leaves **landlord NPV −HK$28,786**, tenant NPV +HK$9,092. Landlord break-even recovery is HK$11,549/year, above all savings. Reduce costs, obtain genuine support, establish valuable residual benefits or refuse. The strongest counterargument is that comfort, safety, customer requirements and family values can justify negative energy-only NPV. That is valid when the decision-maker explicitly accepts and budgets the non-financial benefit; it does not turn hypothetical savings into measured results.

## Climate and ageing require cash tests, not automatic exit dates

### Connect the hazard to the asset and insurance contract

Use **hazard → exposure → vulnerability → financial consequence**. HKO's 158.1mm hourly rain at headquarters on **7 September 2023** demonstrates an observed extreme, not a probability at either premises. HKO's 2025 review records 53 very hot days and 54 hot nights; its SSP2-4.5 projection is a **2041–2060 average of 65 very hot days**, likely range 42–100, not a 2037 forecast. Heat belongs in cooling loads, comfort, stock conditions and worker operations as well as health planning. ([HKO event account](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/education/friends_hko/e-newsletter/vol91/rainstorm_tc_2023.html), [2025 review](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/wxinfo/pastwx/2025/ywx2025.htm), [projection](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/climate_change/proj_hk_annual_vhot_days_info.htm))

DSD's current **Flood-Prone Areas** framework replaces the historical flooding-blackspots list and includes blockage, drainage works and coastal pathways. CEDD's selected coastal protection areas are also not an exhaustive property safety certificate. Without addresses/floors and inspection, neither premises can be declared low risk. Check water-entry paths, loading bays, stock heights, roof/windows, common electrical plant/pumps/lifts, access roads and continuity arrangements. Even an upper-floor unit, if later confirmed, can lose access or utilities. ([DSD](https://www.dsd.gov.hk/EN/Flood_Prevention/Our_Flooding_Situation/Flood_Prone_Areas/index.html), [CEDD](https://www.cedd.gov.hk/eng/our-projects/topics-in-focus/index-id-39.html))

“Basic home insurance” is insufficient to determine adequacy. Home/contents and building/fire cover protect different interests. Compulsory OC insurance is **third-party risks** cover, minimum HK$10M per event from 1 January 2011; it does not prove first-party rebuilding cover for common parts. For the company, inspect stock/fit-out, insured perils, deterioration exclusions, deductibles, underinsurance, gross-profit definition, indemnity period, material-damage trigger, access/utilities and temporary premises. A February 2026 MSIG brochure illustrates why: basic BI additional expenditure differs from optional gross-profit cover, and access/utility extensions have more-than-48-hour conditions. It is an example, not a recommendation or a statement of Carmen's policy. ([IA home guide](https://www.ia.org.hk/en/home_insurance/index.html), [HAD compulsory cover](https://www.buildingmgt.gov.hk/en/Policy_and_Legislation/2_4.html), [MSIG brochure, pp.5–6](https://www.msig.com.hk/sites/msig_hk/files/H711_Business%20Insurance%20_Brochure_Feb%202026_0.pdf))

**Illustrative liquidity stress:** a home levy HK$100,000 plus temporary accommodation HK$20,000 requires **HK$120,000 immediately**. A hypothetical HK$70,000 recovery six months later leaves HK$50,000 permanent cost before interest; at 5% annual discounting, PV cost is HK$51,687. The reserve must bridge gross cash, not just final net loss. Separately, company repairs/stock HK$200,000, temporary operations HK$100,000 and lost cash contribution HK$60,000 create a **HK$360,000 cash shortfall relative to uninterrupted operations**; later recovery HK$200,000 leaves HK$160,000 net. Half is HK$80,000 attributable economic loss under the ownership assumption, **not an automatic personal capital call**. Avoid adding unchanged wages/rent twice or duplicating lost contribution in company value and dividends. No loss likelihood or actual policy recovery is asserted.

A discretionary mitigation costing HK$20,000 over five years at 5% requires **HK$4,619 annual expected net benefit**: `20000/sum(1.05^−t,t=1..5)`. Establish effectiveness and frequency before treating avoided loss as expected benefit. Safety/statutory repairs have their own necessity; a negative energy NPV is not permission to defer them. No reviewed evidence supplies a Wong-specific premium increase, insurance withdrawal or mortgage margin change.

### Building age is a review trigger, not a disposal order

On the 1996 assumption, the building is approximately **30 in 2026, 41 in 2037 and 50 in 2046**, subject to dates. MBIS applies to relevant private buildings age 30+ **served statutory notices**, not automatically an outstanding notice for every such building. HKMA expressly states it sets no specific mortgage-term limit by property age; banks assess commercial/condition factors. HKMC considers RMP properties above 50 case by case, with approved precedents and possible inspection reports. **Withdraw the compulsory 2045 exit recommendation.** Seek actual inspection, repair funding and lender indications; distinguish bank policy, MIP, HKMA and RMP rules. ([BD MBIS](https://www.bd.gov.hk/en/safety-inspection/mbis/index.html), [LCQ17, 10 September 2025](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202509/10/P2025091000373.htm), [LCQ22, 14 May 2025](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202505/14/P2025051300589.htm))

The strongest case against early exit is a sound, well-maintained building, sufficient liquid capital, acceptable lending and a family that values staying. Moving creates cost and disruption. Documented serious defects, an unfunded levy, excluded essential cover, repeated damage or rejected finance would strengthen earlier action. District-level climate evidence alone cannot decide.

## Compare housing choices with debt and equity together

HKMA's December 2023 district research estimates **4–21% lower prices around 2050** in its high scenario under particular ageing assumptions; it explicitly is not a forecast of actual nominal price declines. It does not establish a causal green premium, a 2037 Tai Kok Tsui haircut or an individual repair bill. The newer banking stress-test PDF was not independently retrieved in this pass; old-note figures from it remain unverified here. Overseas insurance withdrawals or detached-house retrofit paybacks are not transferred into this Hong Kong flat/leased warehouse. ([Original HKMA research, RM05/2023](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/media/eng/publication-and-research/research/research-memorandums/2023/RM05-2023.pdf))

For joint RMP in 2037, simple case-year arithmetic makes **Carmen 60 and Adrian 65**; obtain birthdays/closing date. Use the **youngest borrower and two-borrower schedule**. The March 2026 life table gives HK$1,810 fixed or HK$1,650 floating monthly per HK$1M specified property value at youngest age 60. The fixed table assumes 4% interest for the first 30 years, then HKMC Prime minus 2%. At 68 the fixed payout is HK$2,470, a **36.46%** rise per specified value, but waiting also sacrifices eight years of draws and changes portfolio withdrawals. These are today's schedules, not 2037/2045 quotes. ([HKMC payout table, March 2026](https://www.hkmc.com.hk/files/product_file/1/1470/E_Level%20of%20Monthly%20Payout%20and%20Maximum%20Lump-Sum%20Payout%20Amount%20at%20Closing%20(March%202026).pdf))

Suppose, solely illustratively, the decision-date appraisal is HK$11.5M, mortgage is separately discharged, and no fees/lump sums reduce payout. The formula gives specified value `8M+0.5×(11.5M−8M)=HK$9.75M`; current fixed life arithmetic is **HK$17,647.50/month**, HK$211,770/year. A hypothetical 10% appraisal drop to HK$10.35M reduces specified value to HK$9.175M, **5.90% less**. This shows a valuation breakpoint's payout effect; it is no general climate hedge. Maintenance/habitability remain, home equity falls and debt accumulates. ([HKMC programme](https://www.hkmc.com.hk/eng/our_business/reverse_mortgage_programme.html))

RMP draws are borrowing proceeds. The current pack specifies compound interest, a 1.96% specified-value premium in seven 0.28% instalments at months 37,49,…109, plus monthly premium at 1.25% annually of outstanding debt. Existing mortgage payoff can be financed through a closing lump sum, reducing payout; a separately discharged mortgage must not be counted twice. Level payments retain inflation risk. Redemption/sale proceeds settle debt, surplus returns, and insured shortfall protection applies; inspect residence, maintenance, insurance and termination terms. Use a lender loan schedule for terminal debt rather than a simplified compound-interest approximation. ([HKMC information pack, revised March 2026](https://www.hkmc.com.hk/files/product_shortcut/1/79/eng/E_HKMC%20RMP%20Info%20Pack.pdf))

An equally illustrative debt-free downsize from HK$11.5M to HK$8M, with assumed 1% agency on each transaction and HK$50,000 legal/moving/fit-out, releases **HK$3.015M** after current-law HK$240,000 AVD. It retains an HK$8M home and incurs HK$485,000 friction. The AVD uses the 3% band effective **26 February 2026**, not a forecast of 2037 tax. Any existing debt reduces release once. ([IRD current AVD table](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/faq/avd.htm))

**Model handoff:** start every branch with identical liquid assets, appraisal and mortgage; use the same dates, spending/return paths and housing-service requirements. Keep includes debt service, management, insurance and repairs. Retrofit adds incremental costs/savings and residual service value. Downsize includes both transactions, stamp duty, bridge finance, fit-out, moving, replacement-home costs and equity. RMP includes debt discharge/lump sum, draws, fees/premiums, compound debt and maintenance. At a common horizon report liquid funds, consumption funded, shortfalls and **home equity net of debt and selling costs**, including survivor housing. Use matching nominal/real conventions. Test old-home-only and both-home price shocks; they yield different release. Do not re-use the old exploratory success percentages or call gross portfolio legacy total inheritance.

The main remaining uncertainty is site and contract evidence, not the framework. Drainage investment and maintained buildings can weaken the case for moving; measured high use can strengthen retrofit economics; customer or family priorities can justify paying for benefits omitted by an energy-only calculation. Those are legitimate reasons to change a decision. A label, unverified renewal promise or unsupported climate haircut is not equivalent evidence.
