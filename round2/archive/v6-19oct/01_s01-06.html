LOOKBUA: Thank you, Pete. The first gap first. The family's largest risk is losing an income, not a life: neither parent has disability cover. [▶] So disability income cover comes first, 65% of pay to retirement; then a term-life top-up, critical-illness cover of HK$1.5 million each, individual medical cover before group cover ends, and key-person cover for Carmen's business. [▶] In year one it costs about HK$100K, 14% of their surplus, and it's term cover, because the need ends at retirement. [▶] Within three months the parents sign mirror wills with a guardian for Chloe, powers of attorney and advance directives, for under HK$25K. Fahtai.
