## 1. Correct the claims before adding more indicators

It does not prescribe a universal household materiality percentage or supply verification procedures. The current Scope 2 guidance is the **2015 edition**, with corrections listed in 2022. Its electricity-attribute requirements matter where market-based claims are made. The dated **16 October 2026** update describes further development after consultation; hourly matching, deliverability, incrementality and possible revised methods remain proposals, with important requirements unresolved. They must not be presented as already-effective rules. **PCAF Part A, third edition, December 2025**, concerns financial institutions' attributed emissions. Its fluctuation-analysis guidance recognises changes unrelated to investee decarbonisation; its data-quality scale runs from 1, best, to 5, worst. Borrow that discipline, without claiming a household PCAF reporting obligation. **ISSB amendments issued 11 December 2025 apply to annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027**, with early application disclosed. ## 3. Reconcile results before claiming causes

### Compare the same financial job and count each cost once

**Recommendation.** Agree a purchasable conventional twin before measurement, separately for each parent's mandate and then consolidate. Match allocation, geography, credit, duration, economic currency/hedging, liquidity, reinvestment, tax/estate constraints, contribution/withdrawal dates and rebalancing. A green-bond fund and global aggregate fund often perform different jobs; disclose imperfect matching. Ryan's assets remain his. Use time-weighted returns for strategy comparison, and actual cash flows/terminal wealth or money-weighted returns for family experience. These principles borrow from GIPS, without asserting GIPS compliance. [GIPS handbook, effective 1 January 2020](https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/).

For one matched period, let `r_EI/r_PI` be sustainable/parent index returns and `r_EF/r_PF` the corresponding fund NAV total returns. Define wrapper differences `d_E=r_EF−r_EI` and `d_P=r_PF−r_PI`. The exact identity is:

`r_EF − r_PF = (r_EI − r_PI) + d_E − d_P`.

The first term is index-design difference, not identified ESG alpha. Wrapper gaps can already include fees, tax, replication, cash and lending effects. Explain supported components and retain an unexplained residual. Add dealing, platform, custody or adviser costs only where excluded already. For nested conventional policy `T`, matched sustainable policy `S` and actual implementation `A`, the telescoping identity is `r_A−r_T=(r_S−r_T)+(r_A−r_S)`. It describes an observed package difference, not proof of causal ESG treatment. Link multiperiod returns geometrically and reconcile HK$ ending wealth under common cash flows.

**Illustration: one nominal year, HK$1,000,000 starting in each alternative, reinvested income, no external cash flow.** Conventional/sustainable index returns are 5.0%/4.5%; fund NAV returns are 4.8%/4.2%, already reflecting embedded fund costs. Assume external charges of HK$2,000/HK$4,000 paid at year end solely for simple arithmetic. Ending wealth is HK$1,046,000 versus HK$1,038,000. The **HK$8,000 relative shortfall** reconciles to HK$5,000 index-design difference, HK$1,000 differential wrapper effect and HK$2,000 differential external charges. Adding a fund-management fee already reflected in NAV would double count. Neither this cost nor these returns describe the Wongs or a real product.

For unhedged USD returns, conversion is `(1+r_USD) × (HKD-per-USD_end / HKD-per-USD_start) − 1`, using actual dated FX. A trading currency is not the underlying portfolio's economic currency. Do not turn a gap between annualised returns into an asserted historical HK$ annual loss by multiplying today's balance.

Only use detailed sector attribution when beginning weights, consistent sector total returns and trade/linking data support reconciliation. Under single-period Brinson–Fachler assumptions, allocation `Σ(wP−wB)(rB_sector−rB_total)`, selection `ΣwB(rP_sector−rB_sector)` **and interaction** `Σ(wP−wB)(rP_sector−rB_sector)` reconcile the gap. Alternatively combine selection/interaction and name the convention. Public end-date weights are insufficient. Factor regressions describe exposures; they do not identify an ESG causal effect. Start with two net-return lines and a sound cost bridge.

### Separate energy, tariffs, emissions factors and operating conditions

**Illustrative algebra**, one annual period in nominal HK$, not family data. With energy `Q`, flat tariff `T` and emissions factor `F`, simplified bill `B=QT` and emissions `E=QF` give:

`ΔB = T0ΔQ + Q0ΔT + ΔQΔT`.

`ΔE = F0ΔQ + Q0ΔF + ΔQΔF`.

Omitting interaction fails reconciliation. A two-factor symmetric alternative assigns half to each: `ΔB=((T0+T1)/2)ΔQ+((Q0+Q1)/2)ΔT`. This is an accounting convention, not causal evidence. Real blocks, fixed/demand charges, fuel adjustment, subsidies and tax require the actual invoice function.

Assume baseline/current energy of 100,000/80,000 kWh, tariff HK$1.50/1.65 per kWh and factor 0.50/0.45 kgCO2e per kWh. The bill falls HK$150,000→132,000, while calculated emissions fall 50→36 tonnes.

| Raw reconciliation | Quantity effect | Tariff/factor effect | Interaction | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bill change | −HK$30,000 | +HK$15,000 | −HK$3,000 | **−HK$18,000** |
| Emissions change | −10 t | −5 t | +1 t | **−14 t** |

That does not establish project savings. Suppose a separately fitted baseline model predicts **110,000 kWh without the intervention at current weather/activity**, an invented counterfactual here. Against actual 80,000 kWh, estimated intervention saving is 30,000 kWh: gross HK$49,500 at current tariff and a 13.5-tonne inventory-factor equivalent. The following sequential bridge absorbs interactions into later steps; do not add an interaction row again.

| Sequential step, order disclosed | Bill change | Emissions change |
|---|---:|---:|
| Apply current tariff/factor to old 100,000 kWh | +HK$15,000 | −5 t |
| Adjust for weather/activity to 110,000 kWh | +HK$16,500 | +4.5 t |
| Apply intervention to reach 80,000 kWh | −HK$49,500 | −13.5 t |
| **Total observed change** | **−HK$18,000** | **−14 t** |

The 13.5 tonnes are an adjusted operational estimate, not automatically lifecycle or marginal system-wide avoided emissions. A credible system claim needs a suitable alternative, temporal/geographic factors and embodied effects. Gross bill savings also need capex, finance, maintenance, tax and replacement to become net financial outcomes. For actual nonlinear bills calculate `Tariff_current(Q_counterfactual)−Tariff_current(Q_actual)`.

**Recommended measurement plan.** Obtain a full operating/weather cycle with sufficient bills, meter information and relevant activity; fit and freeze a defensible baseline before evaluating; inspect estimated bills, model error, occupancy/hours, rebound and simultaneous improvements. LBNL's BETTER method demonstrates temperature-adjusted baseline analysis, but does not prove it fits this warehouse. Small projects can justify equipment-level measurement rather than unstable whole-building regression. Report prediction, meter and factor uncertainty and an unexplained residual; an observational model alone cannot prove causality. [LBNL method](https://better.lbl.gov/how_it_works/).

For fleet comparisons collect kilometres, payload/service, litres, charging kWh, routes and downtime; compare the same service against a plausible compliant replacement, rather than perpetual use of the old asset. For leased premises establish landlord consent, meter boundary, lease horizon, ownership and who captures the saving before crediting retirement income.

**Portfolio-emissions illustration.** Issuer emissions of 100,000 tonnes, EVIC of HK$1,000 million and a holding of HK$1 million imply 100 attributed tonnes under that simplified listed-equity formula. Holding emissions and investment value constant while EVIC rises to HK$1,250 million produces 80 tonnes, despite no physical reduction. This denominator illustration is not a forecast; actual holding values can also change. Reconcile issuer emissions, holdings, denominators, allocation, FX, coverage and methods separately. An accounting attribution is not evidence that the family caused decarbonisation.

### Behavioural safeguards address the advice process, not presumed defects in the family

| Risk and original evidence | Practical safeguard proposed | Check and limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Present bias/inertia.** Thaler–Benartzi's advance-commitment workplace programme combines several design elements; their separate effects were not isolated. [2004 paper](https://ab-d.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/thaler2004.pdf) | Agree a next task, owner, date and source of payment; simplify collection and use reminders. Only commit future giving after affordability and consent. | Track completion/reasons for delay. Do not use irrevocable giving as a behavioural trick; workplace evidence does not prove household efficacy. |
| **Ambiguity aversion.** Ellsberg distinguishes ambiguous probabilities from known risk without declaring such caution foolish. [1961 paper](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~bskyrms/bio/readings/ellsberg.pdf) | Give both options the same horizon and uncertainty categories; separate estimated risk from unknown impact; offer a reversible pilot where suitable. | Ask each person to explain uncertainty back. Rejecting unverifiable claims can be prudent. |
| **Social desirability/image.** Randomised visibility affects charitable effort in Ariely–Bracha–Meier. [2009 paper](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3130/AER_March2009_v99n1p544.pdf) | Obtain private-first, neutrally framed preferences with none/unsure allowed; then discuss concrete costs, liquidity and outcomes together. Separate values from expected financial gains. | Private/joint differences are not proof of deception. A hypothetical exercise is stated preference, not an executed willingness-to-pay decision. |
| **Loss aversion/framing.** Prospect theory describes reference-dependent risky choices. [1979 paper](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf) | Pre-agree downside responses; show overall goal funding, absolute drawdown and twin difference on one horizon. | **Mental accounting.** Thaler describes departures from fungibility and useful self-control roles. [1999 paper](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/thaler-jbdm1999.pdf) | Keep ownership/accountability sleeves but consolidate exposures, fees, donations and protected goals. | Reconcile totals; avoid presenting ESG or charity money as exempt from opportunity cost. Genuine social preferences remain a strong alternative to signalling: the real pension-policy voting evidence in Bauer–Ruof–Smeets supports that distinction. These studies were not conducted on this family, and no cited paper establishes a retirement benefit from this exact process. Check comprehension, delays and decision consistency in practice, then simplify if burden outweighs value. [Get Real!, 2021](https://www.fir-pri-awards.org/wp-content/uploads/Article_Bauer-Ruof-Smeets.pdf).

### Tax deduction is not a flat rebate

IRD requires an approved charitable donation of **money** to qualifying charity/Government, with aggregate minimum HK$100 and a ceiling of 35% of the relevant assessable income/profits. Salaries-tax calculations and spouse claims have specific rules; receipts must be retained for six years after the assessment year. The charity guide distinguishes public benefit from restricted family/employer benefits. Provider marketing about securities/crypto gifts cannot override the Hong Kong money-only deduction rule. [IRD overview](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/ach.htm); [FAQ](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/faq/acd.htm); [charity tax guide, June 2023](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/pdf/tax_guide_for_charities.pdf).

Calculate `tax liability without gift − tax liability with allowable gift`, for the actual donor and assessment year. The income/profits base, marginal bands, standard-rate comparison, allowances, other gifts and timing matter. Carmen's case income combines salary/dividend and is not all demonstrated taxable salary. Company donations are not automatically parental deductions; do not pool household income into one ceiling. No relevant liability can mean zero saving, while retirement does not prove all relief ceases. A contribution and later distribution of the same charitable money cannot generate two deductions. Treat uncomputed relief as zero in the conservative funding case, rather than booking a refund early.

### Match the promise to cash, ownership and time

**Illustrations only: nominal HK$, no family affordability claim, no provider offer and no guaranteed return.** A first-period gross envelope of **HK$30,000** can fund only the accepted scope it actually buys, including charges. Before transfer call it proposed; after acceptance/payment evidence call it paid. If the quote exceeds the envelope, reduce scope or obtain fresh consent. A household reserve is useful preparation but remains family-owned and revocable; a charity-held programme requires an actual accepted transfer/agreement.

Five end-year awards of HK$30,000 require **HK$150,000 at zero net return before fees**; escalating the first HK$30,000 award by an illustrative 3% annually totals HK$159,274. Twenty fixed nominal awards require **HK$600,000 before fees**; the analogous 3% escalation totals HK$806,111. The old team's illustrative HK$480,000 corpus supplies only HK$24,000 annually over twenty years at zero return before fees. An expected-return present value is a scenario price, not secured payouts. Inflation, sequence, provider and investment risks remain. These arithmetic examples are exploratory support, not exported model claims for slides.

Before an irreversible gift, identify the donor/payment source and run the approved financial plan with actual gross timing, fees, realistic tax timing and a no-tax/no-investment-return case. Check education, healthcare, emergency liquidity and retirement without charity assets. The family must choose an acceptable sacrifice; this brief invents no affordability probability threshold. Once transferred irrevocably, remove capital from household retirement liquidity. No automatic future top-up; new optional gifts can pause if capacity deteriorates, while binding accepted awards must be honoured or varied only through agreed lawful terms.

A funded programme agreement needs purpose, eligibility, conflicts/selection authority, start/end, nominal/real amounts, fees and investment risks, reporting, permitted changes, unspent funds, provider failure/merger and donor incapacity/succession provisions. A public DAF page is not an agreed twenty-year payout contract. The charity retains its independent obligations.

Track **capital transferred → charity administration → award/equipment delivered → access/participation → educational outcome**. Reconcile opening charity assets plus receipts plus net investment result minus fees and grants to closing assets. Label provider-reported delivery separately from independently checked evidence. Awards and installed equipment are outputs; attendance/completion are outcomes influenced by other causes. Ask what would happen without this gift and whether other funding is displaced. Selection-biased recipient/nonrecipient comparisons cannot prove additional attainment. Report paid outputs now, observed outcomes later and causal benefit only with credible evaluation; philanthropy provides no promised financial return to the family.

### Strongest objections, tests and immediate handoff

**Practitioner objection: a careful planner already does suitability, reviews and implementation.** This worries us materially. The claimed innovation must be a decision changed by evidence, price or uncertainty, not the renaming of normal professional work. Demonstrate one corrected cost comparison, one project result with a credible alternative and one honest funded promise.

**Proportionality objection: measurement can cost more than it changes.** Also serious. Prefer available bills, holdings and charity statements; keep attributed portfolio tonnes in the technical annex if they are not decision-useful. Do not commission causal scholarship evaluation or corporate-style assurance solely to decorate a small grant. A reliable low-cost provider dataset, material unexplained investment gap or contractual performance claim would justify deeper work.

**Values objection: a smaller gift or uncertain impact can still be worthwhile.** Agreed. Uncertainty does not erase genuine preferences. Present the cost and evidence honestly, seek informed consent and avoid pretending that donation size or a large endowment makes the advice innovative. A signed economical multi-year charity offer plus acceptable retirement stress results would change the recommendation toward a longer funded term.
