How we think

PRISM

Look both ways.

A green label answers one question: is it green? PRISM asks the five that matter to the family.

ESG ✓

a yes or no

P

Profile · who decides, and within what limits?

Each person's limits and a values budget, set first

instead of asking whether they like ESG

R

Risk · what can climate do to their money?

The whole family's wealth tested in one climate shock

instead of rating each product

I

Impact · what does their money do to the world?

Report the footprint; claim only real changes as impact

instead of calling a lower footprint impact

S

Select · which options are worth it, both ways?

Price each option in HK$ and tonnes; the limits decide

instead of filling a 25% ESG quota

M

Monitor · when do we decide again?

Each decision comes with a trigger to look again

instead of a yearly review
