                  The Lee Family's Journey ­ Navigating Sustainability, Web3 & Retirement

Background

The Lee family resides in Hong Kong and consists of four members:
    1. Rafael Lee (52), the husband and father: Head of fintech at a digital bank.
    2. Fion Lee (46), the wife and mother: Owner of a boutique ESG consulting firm.
    3. Paris (20), the daughter: Year 2 business student at a local university.
    4. Pedro (16), the son: Secondary school student, aspiring to study computer science abroad.

Rafael and Fion's combined annual income is approximately HK$1,800,000. They live in a self-owned
apartment in Tseung Kwan O (market value: HK$8,000,000, outstanding mortgage: HK$2,500,000), and
have aggregate savings and investments of HK$6,000,000, split across traditional asset classes (stocks,
bonds, mutual funds, and cash), with 5% recently allocated to virtual assets (cryptocurrencies and VA
spot ETFs).

Fion's ESG consulting firm, established five years ago, is valued at HK$4,000,000 (business value as a
going concern). The firm is increasingly advising clients on ESG integration, green finance, and
sustainable supply chains.

The Lees have been increasingly concerned about:
    1. The impact of climate change and global demographic shifts on their own long-term financial
         security.
    2. The volatility and regulatory uncertainty of new asset classes, such as virtual assets.
    3. Ensuring their children have the resources for education and future careers in a fast-changing
         world.
    4. How can their investments align with their values (ESG) without sacrificing returns?

New Trends & Developments

The Hong Kong Government, on 26 June, issued the Policy Statement 2.0 on the Development of Digital
Assets in Hong Kong, reinforcing its commitment to establishing Hong Kong as a global hub for
innovation in the digital asset (DA) field. Since the beginning of 2025, Hong Kong has further encouraged
the development of virtual assets, introducing new regulatory frameworks for virtual asset exchanges
and tokenized securities, while also actively promoting the city as a regional hub for sustainable finance.

Inspired by her work and the global momentum on sustainability, Fion wishes to invest in ESG-linked
products and explore new opportunities in virtual assets. Rafael is more conservative, wary of the risks,
but open to new ideas for wealth diversification. Both are interested in how ESG and virtual assets can
be integrated into retirement and legacy planning.

Family's Financial Goals

    1. Retirement Planning: Rafael and Fion wish to retire at ages 65 and 62, respectively, and maintain
         a comfortable lifestyle (estimated post-retirement annual expenses: HK$500,000, inflation-
         adjusted).

    2. Education Funding: Ensure Paris graduates are debt-free and provide for Pedro's university
         education (local or overseas).

    3. ESG Integration: Align at least 20% of their investment portfolio with ESG principles.
    4. Virtual Assets: Explore the potential role of virtual assets in long-term wealth accumulation, but

         require prudent risk management.
    5. Wealth Protection: Safeguard assets and prepare for unforeseen events (critical illness, disability,

         legacy for children).
    6. Succession & Philanthropy: Consider setting up a charitable foundation or trust to support

         environmental causes and family legacy.

Relevant Market Trends & Data

    1. FSTB issued Policy Statement 2.0 on the Development of Digital Assets in Hong Kong.
    2. SFC now licenses 11 virtual asset trading platforms and has issued guidance on virtual asset ETFs

         and tokenized securities. New types of licenses for virtual asset dealing and custodians are
         undergoing public consultation.
    3. HKMA now regulates stablecoins and will issue stablecoin issuer licenses in early 2026.
    4. ESG investments have outperformed traditional indices recently, but concerns remain about
         greenwashing and regulatory inconsistencies.
    5. Possible interest rate cut, moderate inflation rate, increasing local unemployment rate,
         geopolitical tensions heightened, and global economic uncertainty persists.
    6. Insurance & annuity products begin to include ESG options and tokenized payout structures.

Questions

As a financial planner, your role is to guide the Lee family to develop a comprehensive financial plan that
addresses:

    1. Their retirement and education funding needs
    2. Effective integration of ESG principles into their investments
    3. The risks and opportunities of virtual assets as an asset class
    4. Legacy, protection, and philanthropy objectives

Your recommendations should address:
    1. Retirement savings and liquidity strategies
    2. Investment portfolio which incorporates traditional, ESG, and virtual assets asset classes
    3. Protection and insurance solutions
    4. Education funding strategies
    5. Succession, legacy, and charitable planning

You are encouraged to think outside the box and come up with innovative solutions, such as green
bonds, ESG ETFs, tokenized securities, sustainable annuities, decentralized finance (DeFi) products for
yield generation, and digital asset custody.
