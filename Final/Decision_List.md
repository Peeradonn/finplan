# The decision list: what the framework changes

**Status:** draft, 5 Nov 2026, compiled from the five research briefs in `Final/research/` and the Round One plan
(`document/manuscript.md`). For Pete (and the owner of each area). It is the source for slides 2, 5 and 12
(`Final_Plan.md` §4.5) and the list of price tags the model runs (`Final/model_runs.md`).

**The baseline is the label approach** (`Final_Plan.md` §2): what a careful planner does by default, which is what our
Round One did, so its advice fills the "before" column. On slides, say "the label approach", never "October".

**How to read it.** Each decision shows the label approach ("before"), the framework's advice, its corner (`Final_Plan.md` §4.2),
its two-sided price tag (money side, world side), the capital and who pays, when, and the evidence status. Money at
the family level (the odds the money lasts, the inheritance) comes from the model and is filled in from
`Final/model_runs.md`; everything else comes from the briefs, with their tags (SOURCED, ESTIMATED, ILLUSTRATIVE). The
business is our illustrative HK$35M-revenue distributor, Carmen owning 50% (a team decision still open).

**Corners:** DO (good for their money, changes something real) · FINANCE (good for their money, changes nothing real:
do it, don't call it green) · PAY (costs money, changes something real or reflects their values: within a budget set in
advance) · REFUSE (costs money or credibility, changes nothing real).

## 3. Each decision's price tag

Money at the family level (odds, inheritance) is in `Final/model_runs.md`; the lines below give what the briefs
established and point to the run.

### B1. Electric vans, LED, office air-con (`area2_business.md` §3c–3d)
- **Money (business):** saves ≈HK$372K a year before tax (vans HK$344K at the HK$37.93/L diesel list price; ≈HK$212K at
  HK$25/L), payback 5.1 years on full capex, year-1 cash after debt service +HK$23.6K, debt cover 5.7× → 3.3×. The
  electric van costs ≈HK$12.4K more than diesel after the first-registration-tax waiver, ≈HK$83K more if the waiver
  lapses after 31 Mar 2028.
- **Money (family):** through the sale price (extra profit × the sale multiple × Carmen's share × the plan's 60% haircut)
  and, as a variant, through dividends. Run M2.
- **World:** 26.1 t a year (vans 19.3, LED 4.1, air-con 2.8), 54% of Scope 1+2; additional (her own operations);
  measured once running (fuel receipts, meter).
- **Green finance:** a green label on the loan only if free (self-certified or the bank's SME framework); net greenium
  ≈HK$0 (B2). **CHECK:** a fleet diesel price.

### B2. Refuse a sustainability-linked loan and paid green labels at this size (`area2_business.md` §3d; `area2_supply_chain.md` §3c)
- **Money:** a ratchet on HK$0.9M is worth HK$450–4,500 a year against ≈HK$12K a year of verification; it breaks even only
  above HK$4.8M of debt at 25bp. A 25bp green discount on HK$1.5M saves ≈HK$12K over five years, the cost of the
  cheapest assessment. The HKMA grant needs at least HK$100M (`hook_fact_checks.md`). Bank supply-chain finance: anchors
  at ≈US$100M of trade spend; her purchases are ≈US$3.4M.
- **World:** none: the vans happen either way.

### B3. Emissions data (`area2_business.md` §3e; `area2_supply_chain.md` §3c)
- **Money (business):** costs ≈HK$54–73K a year. Protects ≈HK$209K of Carmen's stake per 1% of sales kept; pays for
  itself at 0.6% of sales. Her listed customers report Scope 3 from FY2026 (LargeCap; `hook_fact_checks.md`).
- **Money (family):** a sensitivity, not a forecast: if a customer worth 5% or 10% of sales leaves for a supplier with
  better data, the sale proceeds fall. Run M3.
- **World:** no tonnes by itself; it is what makes B4 and supplier choices measurable.
- **Evidence:** no study measures an SME sale premium for emissions data; we model value through revenue kept, not a
  multiple premium.

### B4. Recycled content (`area2_supply_chain.md` §4)
- **Money (business):** ≈HK$0 at Oct 2026 resin parity in Southeast Asia (search extract: CHECK); up to ≈HK$151K a year
  at the 2023 peak, passed to customers as an itemised line (1.4 HK cents a cup). Not the family's values budget.
- **World:** ≈108 t a year (Scope 3, purchased goods; ESTIMATED with DESNZ factors).
- **Caveat:** Phase 2 of the plastics rules would ban plastic tableware regardless; aim recycled content at
  pre-packaged and retail packaging, fibre at tableware.

### B5. Stop "compostable" claims (`area2_supply_chain.md` §2b; `area2_business.md`)
- **Money:** protects the tableware stock exposed to the plastics rules (≈HK$0.77M in the business brief: CHECK the
  basis) and the claims risk.
- **World:** no tonnes; it stops a label that misleads. The greenwashing control on Carmen's own business.

### B6. Green lease at the 2027 renewal (`area4_property_scenario.md` §4)
- **Money:** a secure lease to the 2035–39 sale is worth more than the kWh; LED savings are B1's.
- **World:** small. Clauses: reinstatement waiver, shared data, cost recovery capped at measured savings, a renewal
  option to 2039.

### B7. The sale (`area2_business.md` §3e)
- **Money:** keep the HK$3.0M counted, no green uplift. The fleet's second replacement (≈2035–36) falls in the sale
  window: price on a cash-free, debt-free basis. Each HK$1M of price ≈4 points (82.6% base; Run M2 recomputes on the
  plan with the medical review).

### P1–P5. The portfolio (`area3_portfolio.md` §3–5)
- **Money:** fees for both portfolios HK$18,175 → HK$8,528 a year (≈HK$9.6K saved, mostly the wrapper). Against an
  all-plain twin, values cost ≈HK$5.1K a year in fees (0.05% of investable assets). The real price is tracking: ±HK$26.5K
  in a typical year on the HK$1.863M climate sleeve; −HK$26.6K a year if the last five years repeat; ≈0 over 2007–26.
  Run M4.
- **World:** alignment, not impact (shares bought on the market fund nothing new). Carbon intensity ≥27% below an
  all-plain equity book by the index rule; **no measured figure yet** (CHECK: Win).
- **P3:** Adrian's 1.04-point fee gap against a 0–8bp greenium: saves ≈HK$1,076 a year.
- **P4:** Carmen's HK$2,153 a year buys bond-by-bond checks; the fund trails its benchmark by ≈1 point a year since 2017;
  keep only with an impact report and the entry charge waived.
- **Family ESG share:** 27.7% (candidate A) or 24.8% (B).

### P6. The mandate (`area1_6_framework.md` §3c)
- Separate portions; shared money follows the more prudent profile; a 5bp cost test for shared money; Carmen's values
  budget (tracking error ≤3% a year); any choice that takes the household below a floor (ILLUSTRATIVE: 80% to 89) needs
  both signatures; the plain twin is the default while the parents disagree; every choice recorded.

### H1. Releasing the flat (`area4_property_scenario.md` §3.4)
- **Money:** if the old flat is worth 10% less in 2037 (newer flats hold), downsizing releases ≈30% less (HK$4.46M →
  HK$3.13M) and the plan loses ≈6 points; the reverse mortgage pays ≈6% less and loses ≈1.4 points (82.6% base). The
  inheritance: reverse mortgage `{{legacy_rmp}}`, downsizing `{{legacy_down}}`. Run M6 recomputes both measures on the
  plan with the medical review.
- **Trigger:** each year, compare the flat's price per sq ft with a ≈10-year-old flat in the target district; if the gap
  widens by 10% or more, decide the route that year. Release by the building's 50th year.

### H2–H4. Green mortgage, retrofit, the next flat (`area4_property_scenario.md` §3.1–3.2, 3.4)
- H2: no lower rate; HK$0–3K one-off on HK$1.8M, only if the building (or, for HKMC, the flat) is BEAM Plus certified;
  SC and HSBC exclude refinancing (`research/hook_fact_checks.md`).
- H3: ≈HK$1,900 a year; no solar (the roof is the owners' corporation's).
- H4: the one Hong Kong study's 4.4% premium is ≈HK$0.3M on HK$7M, for ≈HK$2K a year of energy savings.

### G1. The scholarship (`area1_6_framework.md` §7)
- **Money:** the label approach's "about HK$30K a year" from cash flow, for life, costs ≈6.9 points (82.6% base) and was never in the
  model. A funded 20-year fund (2 × HK$240K in 2027–28, 17% relief) costs ≈2.6 points. Perpetual only from sale proceeds
  above HK$3.0M: ≈0. Run M5.
- **World:** students funded (≈HK$30K a year for 20 years); additional (it pays for awards that would not exist).
- **Wording fix:** "a tax-exempt charity under s.88", not "a registered charity".

**What this does to the story:**
1. **"Most of what's real also pays" holds in direction.** All ≈134 t a year come from decisions that pay (the vans:
   +HK$0.63M to the inheritance) or cost about nothing (recycled content at resin parity). What costs money is the ESG
   funds' alignment (≈HK$0.57M in fees against plain twins, plus tracking risk), a label kept only as Carmen's values
   choice, and the scholarship (≈HK$0.80M, real and chosen). This supports the upgraded thesis (`Final_Plan.md` §4,
   "what doesn't pay is mostly the label"); it stays EXPECTED until Pete reviews these runs.
2. **A new decision the model found: pay out Carmen's share of the van saving.** Through the sale price alone, the vans
   add ≈HK$0.6M to the inheritance; paid out as dividends while she works, ≈HK$3.3M. The family's plan counts no dividends.
   Whether the company pays it out is a business decision (the co-founder's half too); worth one line on slide 6.
3. **Don't credit sustainability with plan hygiene.** Of the new plan's +HK$2.57M against the label approach, roughly
   HK$1.1M is cheaper fund wrappers and HK$0.8M is funding the scholarship properly; the vans give ≈HK$0.6M. The talk
   should say which is which.
4. **The scenario on the plan as presented:** 96.8% → 89.5%, the median inheritance −HK$4.6M. The portfolio is still the
   largest channel; the route out of the flat decides the rest (downsizing in the scenario: 82.7%, but HK$11.1M median).

**Caveats:** the vans' family effect rests on ILLUSTRATIVE business inputs (5.6× net profit, Carmen's 50%, the diesel
list price); the fee saving compares against the label approach's BOC-Prudential fund, which held no assets and whose 0.775% fee
was never verified, so quote the price of values against plain twins (−HK$0.57M) instead; guardrails are off, as in
`switch_89`.
