# Funded philanthropy and legacy for the Wongs

Research cutoff and access date: 8 November 2026. Verdict: a paid, finite educational gift is feasible in principle; the team's specific HK$480,000, twenty-year named scholarship with 17% relief is not yet a feasible provider-backed recommendation. Recommend an affordable, fully paid first-period grant through an existing charity, with a longer term conditional on a written gift agreement and a rerun of retirement affordability. Do not count a revocable will clause as current funding.

Confidence: high on IRD rules and the existence of the provider routes below; medium on applying a provider to this family without onboarding or an agreement; low on any assertion that the family can afford a particular amount. No provider was contacted and no transaction was made. Figures below are arithmetic illustrations, not shared-model outputs or family measurements. Foreign currency conversions use an explicitly illustrative HK$7.80/US$1, not a verified executable exchange rate.

## What must be corrected in the team's current proposal?

### Takeaway

The case supports considering education or environmental philanthropy, but supplies no scholarship amount, accepted governance roles, donor identity, agreed affordability limit, or twenty-year provider offer. Funding and measuring the commitment must precede any claim about its impact.

### Cited Findings

- The case, p6, asks the family to *consider* a charitable/philanthropic plan connected with education or environmental causes. Area6 of the Final topic, pp3–4, requires a funded philanthropic/legacy allocation and separates financial, sustainability, impact and implementation outcomes. These are primary local documents: [case](../../Primary%20Info/Award2026Case20260919.pdf), [Final topic](../../Final/2026%20UFP%20Final%20Round%20Topic.pdf). The research support text extractions are in `Final/research/area6_support/text/`.
- The old team brief §7 and Decision_List G1 assume twenty years around HK$30,000 a year, HK$480,000 paid in 2027–28, and approximately17% tax relief; they expressly leave charity minimums and fees unverified. These are team assumptions, not provider offers: [framework brief](../../Final/research/area1_6_framework.md), [decision list](../../Final/Decision_List.md).
- An approved charitable donation is money given for charitable purposes to a s88 tax-exempt charity or Government. No material benefit may return to the donor. The IRD guide also rejects scholarships restricted by a common employer/family connection as a general public-benefit route; charitable status and deductible donations are different questions. [IRD Tax Guide, June2023, paras9–11 and54–59](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/pdf/tax_guide_for_charities.pdf).
- IRD's live explanatory page sets a minimum aggregate HK$100 and maximum35% of the applicable assessable income/profits. Its FAQ specifies the salaries-tax ceiling base as income after allowable expenses/depreciation, permits specified spouse claims, and requires receipts retained six years after the assessment year. These pages are undated; current text accessed8Nov2026. [IRD overview](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/ach.htm), [IRD FAQ](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/faq/acd.htm).

### Inferences

- Replace flat17% relief with `tax liability without donation - tax liability with allowable donation`, separately for the actual taxpayer and assessment year. A deduction is not a tax credit. Recompute marginal bands, standard-rate comparison, allowances and ceiling; do not apply household income as one pooled ceiling. The case describes Carmen's720K as salary/dividend, so it cannot all be treated as taxable salary without clarification. A company donation is the company's expense/deduction, not automatically the parents' personal donation or a benefit available solely to Carmen's assumed50% stake.
- Do not state that all relief ends when salaries stop in2039: other assessable profits/income and assessment elections might matter. Conversely, if no relevant liability exists, saving can be zero. Do not book a tax refund before its actual timing is established.
- A deduction for a contribution into a genuine charity-held DAF and a later grant cannot both be claimed for the same money. A household-owned earmarked bank account is still household property; it can be a reserve, but is neither an irrevocable charitable transfer nor proof of an operational twenty-year scholarship.
- The old assertion that selling securities 'costs nothing' is too broad even when no capital-gains tax applies: dealing spreads, fees, FX and possible taxpayer-specific consequences still matter. Avoid adding this claim.

### Gaps

- Actual donor, relevant tax treatment, income base, timing, other gifts and spouse allocation; verified tax computation; agreed family affordability; beneficiary purpose; provider acceptance of restrictions and naming; payment schedule; fees including investments; inflation policy; and enforceability of payouts remain unknown.
- The old research groups a will clause with money set aside under one funded test. Separate 'current grant paid', 'funded future programme', 'household reserve', and 'testamentary intention'.

### Inferences

- Strongest executable direction: select a direct, first-period education grant, quote and approve its gross all-in amount, pay the charity, receive a proper receipt and accepted purpose, and obtain delivery evidence. A fully paid one-year gift satisfies 'funded' for that one-year scope. It does not satisfy an unannounced twenty-year promise. Broaden from design-scholarship to equipment only with family consent; the case allows education generally, but the family has not chosen this particular route.
- If family values demand the sustainable-design scholarship specifically, the plan remains a conditional recommendation until an existing charity agrees to the exact eligibility, award/fee amount, selection control, payout dates, reporting and unused-funds rules. Do not invent a provider. A twenty-year grant can be pre-funded only when the full contractual costs are transferred or otherwise secured under a verified arrangement, with remaining investments and risks clear.
- Do not create a standalone family foundation for this illustration. Governance/legal/accounting/administrative costs were not quoted and the added complexity has no demonstrated benefit at this proposed scale. An existing charity's DAF is a real option, not automatically the cheapest one.

### Gaps

- Provider legal name/s88 status must be checked at donation, actual accepted donation purpose, payment costs, cancellation/refund events, residual-fund destination, insolvency/merger provisions, donor incapacity/succession, exact fee basis and inflation treatment. Public marketing is not an executed offer. All undated live materials describe what was retrieved8Nov2026; historical availability of each line at an earlier date was not reconstructed.
- No source assures a guaranteed net investment return, guaranteed twenty-year award, or binding right for the family to select beneficiaries. 'Named' is not equivalent to 'controlled'.

## How can a finite commitment be funded, protected and measured honestly?

### Takeaway

Start with a fully paid period and an explicit budget that retirement can bear. Use a separate capital reconciliation and evidence chain; an award count is an output, not proof of additional educational achievement.

### Cited Findings

- The competition requires capital, expected outcomes, assumptions and authoritative evidence, and a dashboard with financial, sustainability, impact and implementation outcomes: [Key Areas, pp1–2](../../Final/2026%20UFP%20Final%20Round%20Topic%20Key%20Areas.pdf).
- Provider evidence available from the actual direct education route is procurement progress and installation photography, while Give2Asia describes project reporting. These support delivery verification; neither source promises causal evidence of a child's future attainment. [TWGHs scheme](https://www.tungwah.org.hk/en/fund-raising-news/eeds2026/), [Give2Asia DAF fact sheet](https://give2asiahk.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/8_2026-HK-DAF-Services-Fact-Sheet-ENCN-Traditional.pdf).

### Inferences

**Illustrative worked funding alternatives; no claim of family affordability or quoted provider contract:**

1. First-period direct-gift envelope HK$30,000 paid now, no assumed tax saving or investment return, one stated educational purpose and one reporting period. This is HK$30,000 irreversibly leaving family retirement assets once accepted. If actual equipment plus payment charges exceed that envelope, reduce the scope or obtain fresh approval before transfer. Charity fee absence alone does not establish the net equipment budget. Label 'proposed funded grant' before payment and 'paid/received' only after documentary evidence.
2. Five annual awards ofHK$30,000 nominal, beginning at end ofyear1: the award cash alone isHK$150,000 at zero net return. Add actual separately quoted administration/payment/reporting costs. A charity-held prepaid programme could meet it if accepted on those terms; a family reserve with150K is merely earmarked, not an irrevocably funded charity programme. At illustrative3%annual award escalation, year1 award30K and five awards totalHK$159,274. No inflation forecast is asserted.
3. Twenty fixed nominal awards30K requireHK$600,000 cash at zero net return before fees; if year1 is30K and each later award rises3%, total cashHK$806,111. The old480K corpus divided across20years at zero return providesHK$24,000 yearly before fees. These sums show why an expected-return present value must not be presented as a funding guarantee. An expected-return annuity formula can price a scenario but does not remove market/sequence/inflation/provider risk.
4. For a hypothetical30K donation, gross/net cash cost with no saving is30K; if a separately verified effective tax saving were15%, cost25.5K; at17%,24.9K. Those percentages are sensitivities, not recommendations or findings about either parent. Claim any actual saving only after the exact tax computation.

**Recommended project governance, all roles proposed and subject to acceptance:**

- Both owners consent to use of shared household assets; company money requires its own lawful approval and co-owner treatment. Children may advise on purpose after acceptance of a role; charity makes eligible award decisions under its agreement. Adviser checks affordability and tax inputs but does not invent donor consent or beneficiary evidence.
- Before transferring, run the same retirement plan with actual gross gift timing, realistic tax receipt/timing, fees and a no-tax/no-investment-return case. Confirm liquidity for education, healthcare and retirement commitments. The acceptance threshold is a family decision, not an invented probability floor. Model changes/results belong to the coordinator's export/check process; none were made here.
- Once gifts are irrevocably made, remove their corpus from household liquidity and retirement wealth. Do not fund retirement from charity money. No automatic top-up: future optional grants pause when the family cannot meet its approved financial limits; already binding awards must instead be honoured from their funded pool or varied only by the agreed charity mechanism. Avoid telling an accepted scholar 'we pay less' unless that was validly agreed in advance.
- Review before each grant and at annual family planning. Event review when provider fees/purpose/s88 status change, required report is overdue, beneficiaries cannot be found, fraud/conflict is suspected, investment loss threatens outstanding awards, or household financial capacity changes. Protect paid commitments first, escalate exceptions, stop new discretionary awards and record the decision. Do not invent a fixed30-day,80%coverage or90%assurance rule.
- Gift agreement should fix start/end, nominal or real amount, permitted award changes, administration, selection/conflicts, evidence, unspent funds/charity failure, who may recommend later, and who can amend purpose. It cannot be assumed to override the charity's independent duties.

**Measurement dictionary for the giving row:**

| Item | Definition and baseline | Latest data/quality | Review and action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Funding coverage | Charity-held eligible liquid resources **net of accrued fees** divided by remaining committed payouts plus unavoidable costs; disclose nominal/real basis and date. Separate forecast investment income rather than treating it as cash. Baseline is signed agreement/payment, not assumption480K. | Provider statements reconciled opening assets + receipts + net investment result - fees - grants = closing assets; classify cash vs valuation estimate. | Before next award and annually; projected deficit triggers programme review/no new optional promises, not automatic draw from retirement. |
| Delivery | Cash actually received by charity/recipient or equipment installed; number/value of eligible awards and evidence received. | Receipt, transaction match, de-identified award register or procurement/install evidence; identify provider-reported vs independently checked. | Agreed report dates; missing report means unknown delivery and enquiry, not zero impact or proof of fraud. |
| Outcomes/contribution | Pre-specified plausible result: attendance/retention, access to equipment, completion; compare with baseline and what other support already exists. | Beneficiary consent, privacy-safe reporting, response rates and uncertainty; small samples likely lack robust control groups. | Review whether meaningful outcomes can be measured proportionately; otherwise state 'funded X outputs; causal educational benefit not established'. |
| Implementation | Purpose/taxpayer/provider/agreement/funding/report all evidenced; unknown fields shown explicitly. | Dated source and decision log, proposed owner, completion dates. | Missing legal/fee/purpose facts block a twenty-year commitment claim. |

**Impact chain and attribution:** family capital transferred → charity administers/selects → award/equipment delivered → student access/participation → educational outcome. At each arrow ask what would happen without this gift, whether other funding is displaced, and what else caused the observed change. A fund can pay additional *awards* without demonstrating additional *attainment*. Count funded awards and net grant value now; report outcomes when observed; make a causal claim only with credible evaluation. A comparison to nonrecipients may itself be confounded by selection. Do not call philanthropy an impact investment yielding a family financial return.
