## 2. The portfolio-selection framework (Win's slide 8) — the "Counting Rule"

**The core change from Round One:** "on the SFC ESG list" stops being the test and becomes only an *entry check* for
HK-authorised funds. The real test is three gates (`area3_portfolio.md §3`). **This is "Beyond Green Labels" made
operational.**

A holding counts as sustainable **only if it passes all three:**

| Gate | Equity | Bonds |
|---|---|---|
| **1. A binding, published outcome rule** | An index whose carbon intensity must sit **≥30% below its parent** (EU CTB or stricter), **−7%/yr** | **≥85%** use-of-proceeds bonds, each checked against the four ICMA principles |
| **2. Measured and reported every year** | WACI + coverage | An impact report |
| **3. Within the owner's budgets** | Fee gap **≤5bp** vs the plain twin; tracking error within budget | same |

**Tracking-error budget per person** (`area1_6_framework.md §3c`): Adrian **≤0.5%/yr** (prudent, retires 2037);
Carmen **≤3%/yr** (values, longer horizon).

## 3. The mandate framework (Win's slide 7) — "output, not target"

The ESG share is **not** chosen to hit the case's 20–25%. It falls out of three factors per person:

1. **Time horizon** — Adrian is ~10 yrs from retirement; Carmen is longer.
2. **Tracking-error budget** — Adrian 0.5%/yr; Carmen 3%/yr.
3. **Values / preference** — Adrian prudent; Carmen a values-driven co-founder.

Put the same ESG core inside each budget and the weight *falls out*:
- **Adrian → 30%** of his portfolio (≈¾ of his equity; he keeps 10% plain IWDA).
- **Carmen → 60%** of her portfolio (all her equity).
- **Family = 27.7%** — labelled "output, not target."

## 5. Honesty guardrails (the Area 6 / "measurement trap" point)

A lower attributed carbon figure is **not** proof of a real cut. Portfolio WACI can fall because valuations, holdings or
data coverage changed — the same way an electricity bill can fall on a tariff change, cooler weather or less activity,
not efficiency. So WACI is reported as **exposure the family holds (footprint)**, never as tonnes the family abated
(impact). The only tonnes the family *causes* are Carmen's vans.

**Keep five separate ledgers, never net one against another:** (1) operational inventory, (2) portfolio emissions (WACI),
(3) alignment to a rule (EU CTB −7%/yr), (4) project benefits, (5) charitable outcomes. Mixing them double-counts.
