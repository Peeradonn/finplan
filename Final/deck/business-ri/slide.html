FAHTAI: For any business, Risk and Impact use the same sequence: scan, measure, carry forward. Risk traces environmental causes through customers, products, suppliers, operations and finance. Record the money exposed, the timing and uncertainty. Keep what could change the advice; flag missing evidence. Impact traces purchases through operations to disposal. Estimate the footprint from activity data, state its limits, and ask where the business can control or influence a change. For Carmen, packaging is the connection. On the risk side, check whether customers require product data and which sales depend on it. On the impact side, verify the quantities and supplier footprints behind our estimate.
