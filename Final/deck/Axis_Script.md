## 2 · PRISM
*Pete · 1:00 · 134 spoken words (≈60 s at 135)*

**Pete:** How do we run those two tests? With PRISM. A green label gives one answer: yes or no. PRISM splits it into five questions. Profile: who decides, and within what limits. We set each person's limits, and a budget for their values, before we price anything. Risk is the first test, outside in: the whole family goes through one climate shock. Impact is the second, inside out: we count only real changes, never a cleaner-looking footprint. Select: every decision goes on one measure, green or not. Monitor: every decision comes with a trigger to look again. What comes out is one price tag for every decision: what it does for the children, in Hong Kong dollars, and what it does for the world, in tonnes. Then the limit decides. Here are the Wongs' six.
