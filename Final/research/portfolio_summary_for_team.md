## 2. The three-test counting rule

A holding counts as "sustainable" only if it passes all three:

1. **Binding, published outcome rule.** Equity: index carbon intensity ≥30% below parent (EU CTB or stricter). Bonds:
   ≥85% use-of-proceeds checked against ICMA. No rule → not counted.
2. **Measured and reported each year.** Equity: WACI and coverage. Bonds: impact report (tCO₂e avoided). No data → not
   counted.
3. **Within the owner's budgets.** Fee gap and tracking error stay inside the limits set per person.

"On the SFC list" is an entry check for HK-authorised funds, not the test itself.

**What the corrected numbers actually say — and why this is a stronger slide.**

The fund delivers **30.2%**, against a legal floor of **30%**. It does what the rule requires and essentially nothing
more. That is the finding: *a binding rule buys you exactly the rule.* It is measured, dated, and it is the reason to
check the number rather than trust the label — the same lesson as the BOC-Prudential fund, now with a second piece of
evidence. A team quoting "52%" off a five-year-old baseline would be making the greenwashing mistake the topic is
named after.

The price is now also measured, not inferred: **−1.43 points a year over five years** against the plain index, from
two primary factsheets at the same date in the same currency. This independently confirms the brief's justETF
estimate (also −1.43), which closes open item §7.3 of `area3_portfolio.md`.

So the two-sided price tag for slide 8 is: **30% less carbon, for about 1.4 points a year of return in the worst
five-year window we have.** Both sides sourced. That is the trade-off the brief asks us to show.

**Diversification** still favours EDMW over a screening index: 1,081 holdings vs the Selection index's 632, top 10
27.65% vs 35.05%, and it **keeps** Apple (5.76%), Amazon (2.68%), Broadcom (1.68%) and Meta (1.55%) — optimisation
reweights rather than excludes. For a retirement portfolio that matters more than the label.

**Two rule details worth knowing** (from the regulation itself, not a summary):
- The 30% baseline is the **"investable universe"**, not "the parent index". Don't say parent on the slide.
- CTBs are **not** exclusion-free. Art. 10(2) makes Art. 12(1)(a)–(c) apply to CTBs too from 31 Dec 2022:
  controversial weapons, tobacco, and UNGC/OECD violators. The fossil-fuel exclusions are PAB-only.

**Remaining weakness — Win to close.** The 90.76 comes from **XWD**, a Canadian iShares fund tracking MSCI World, not
from IWDA/SWDA itself: BlackRock prints **no** sustainability section on the plain MSCI World factsheet (SFDR
"Other"). Same MSCI data date and 99.56% coverage make it a sound proxy, but on a slide it must be labelled as a
proxy. To remove the proxy, pull IWDA's own WACI from the iShares **product page** (the script-loaded block, the route
that worked for EDMW), not the factsheet. Also pull the EDMW **Acc** line's 5y return specifically — the 9.13% above
is the USD Dist line (total-return basis, so near-identical, but make it exact).
