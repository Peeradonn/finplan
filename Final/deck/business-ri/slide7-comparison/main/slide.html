FAHTAI: I'm Fahtai, on Carmen's business. For any business: measure the whole footprint, then rank every cut by its cost per tonne, cheapest first. She runs four diesel delivery vans from a leased warehouse, but those are under two per cent of her footprint; the rest is the packaging she buys. So her biggest cut is a purchasing decision: recycled plastic, 108 tonnes a year, at no extra cost while recycled and new plastic cost the same. Swapping her four diesel vans for electric ones saves money, but it's 19 tonnes, and the only cut that needs a loan. And every saving reaches the family: Carmen sells her half of the business in 2035 to 2039, at about five and a half times profit, so what the business saves raises the price. And we stop her compostable claims: in Hong Kong, PLA is plastic with nowhere to compost. So should that one loan be a green one?
