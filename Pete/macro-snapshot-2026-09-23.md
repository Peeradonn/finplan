## 1. What changed since the case was written

**The Fed raised rates on 16 Sep 2026** by 25bp to **3.75–4.00%**, its first hike since 2023, citing oil-driven
inflation. The dot plot signals one more hike in 2026. The **HKMA raised its Base Rate to 4.25%** on 17 Sep
automatically under the peg. The three note-issuing banks **held Prime at 5.00%** (StanChart 5.25%).

Consequences for us:
- Rates are rising again, not falling. Deposit and bond yields are the most attractive they have been in years,
  which strengthens the case for the short-horizon buckets (Chloe's education, emergency reserve).
- Mortgage risk is live again. The family's mortgage and any refinancing option should be stress-tested at Prime.
- Property prices had just ended a 13-month rally. A hiking cycle is a real downside risk to the HK$11.5M flat.
