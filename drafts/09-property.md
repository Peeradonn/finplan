## PROPERTY: KEEP NOW, DECIDE AT 65

**Keep the Tai Kok Tsui home today; at Adrian's retirement in 2037, choose between a reverse mortgage and
downsizing; sign the enduring powers of attorney now so that choice stays open.**

**Refinance? Not now.** The HK$1.8M mortgage costs about 3.5%, close to what cash earns (3.0%), and its interest
is tax-deductible (≈HK$10.7K a year). Repaying it early would lock liquidity in a HK$11.5M asset that is already
61% of the family's wealth with the business. We will compare the current rate with new H-plans, capped at 3.25%,
and switch only if the saving clears the fees.

**Keeping the home untouched is the one option that fails.** With the MPF annuity, lower spending after the first
death and the business sale in place, holding the home as a pure legacy asset leaves a **62%** chance the money
lasts to Carmen's 89 (Figure 14b). Releasing its value is what makes the retirement secure:

| Option at 2037 | Money lasts to Carmen's 89 | Left to heirs at 89 (today's money) |
|---|---|---|
| Keep the home, no release | 62% | HK$10.0M, most of it the flat |
| **Reverse mortgage**, HK$230K a year for life | **85%** | HK$8.6M |
| **Downsize** to a HK$7M flat (releases ≈HK$4.5M) | **85%** | HK$12.8M |

*Source: Team analysis; HKMC Reverse Mortgage Programme terms; model mix 2, 10,000 simulations.*

**A) Downsizing is the stronger financial answer.** It matches the reverse mortgage on security and leaves
≈HK$4M more to Ryan and Chloe, because a sale carries no loan interest or insurance.

**B) The reverse mortgage buys the right to stay.** HKMC's programme pays a fixed income for life, lets both
spouses stay in the home, and is non-recourse: if the loan outgrows the flat, HKMC bears the loss. Its cost is
the legacy, which shrinks to near zero if Carmen reaches 95. Only HK$8M of the flat's value plus half the excess
counts towards the payout.

**C) The decision belongs to the family at 65, with the numbers in front of them.** Which one they choose depends
on whether staying in Tai Kok Tsui matters more than what they leave. Either way, **starting in 2037 matters**:
delaying the reverse mortgage to 2045 costs 8 points of retirement security (§11).
