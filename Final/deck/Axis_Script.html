Test the whole plan, then choose the products.

PeteGood morning, we're Team Axis. I'm Pete. What makes a family's whole plan sustainable? Choosing ESG funds is one decision. ESG analysis already considers investment risks. For the Wongs, a Hong Kong family with a home, a packaging business and retirement ahead, we ask two questions: what does climate do to their money, and what do their decisions change in the world? In our model, their net worth is about three million Hong Kong dollars lower by 2032 than the same plan without that shock. This is a scenario, not a forecast. It shows why we test the whole plan before choosing products. Then we ask what their decisions actually change, and at what cost. That's the job of PRISM.
