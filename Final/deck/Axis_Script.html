PeteGood morning, we're Team Axis. I'm Pete. What makes a plan sustainable? Without a test, the answer is a share: meet the client's ESG wish with part of the savings in labelled funds. But a share only says what the funds hold, not whether the plan works. A plan must pass three tests. Outside in: does it last when climate hits? Inside out: what does the money actually change?
