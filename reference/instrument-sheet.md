# Next-generation instrument sheet — Hong Kong, September 2026

Written for: our competition team.
Compiled 14 September 2026. **Every dated claim below needs re-checking before we submit** — this space
moved substantially between April and June 2026 and will move again before 2 October.

Why this exists: the [2026 poster](2026-Award-Poster-20260725.jpg) names *"green finance, tokenized
securities, and Web3 technologies"* outright, so the case will almost certainly invite these instruments.
Round One gives no marks for novelty — but §4.4 of [PREP.md](../PREP.md) requires us to **defend** each
recommendation, and §4.5 requires **safeguards**. The "HK regulatory status" column is what does that work.

## 1. State of play

### Stablecoins — regime live, two licensees

The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656) commenced **1 August 2025**. The HKMA received **36 formal
applications** by the 30 September 2025 deadline and granted the **first two licences on 10 April 2026**:

| Licence | Licensee | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| FRS01 | Anchorpoint Financial Limited — a JV of Standard Chartered Bank (HK), HKT and Animoca Brands | 10/04/2026 |
| FRS02 | The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited | 10/04/2026 |

Source: [HKMA Register of Licensees](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/regulatory-resources/registers/register-of-licensed-stablecoin-issuers/),
last revised 23 April 2026 — still two licensees at that revision. A ~5.6% approval rate on the first
round. Both licensees indicated product launches in H2 2026; HSBC signalled an HKD-denominated stablecoin.

**Why this matters for the case:** the 2025 case said stablecoin licences were coming "in early 2026."
That is now history, and we can write about a live regime with named licensees rather than a forthcoming one.

### Tokenised investment products — the big 2026 change

On **20 April 2026** the SFC issued the *Circular on Tokenisation of SFC-authorised Investment Products*
and the *Circular on Secondary Trading of Tokenised SFC-authorised Investment Products*
([SFC ref 26EC22](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/api/circular/list-content/circular/doc?lang=EN&refNo=26EC22)).

- Permits **24/7 secondary trading** of tokenised SFC-authorised products.
- Authorises **regulated stablecoins** (fiat-referenced, licensed under the Stablecoins Ordinance) and
  **tokenised deposits** to settle those trades.
- SFC-licensed VATPs may offer secondary trading of tokenised open-ended funds, subject to fair pricing,
  orderly trading, liquidity provision and disclosure requirements.
- As of **March 2026**, **13 tokenised products** were authorised for public offering, with tokenised
  share-class AUM up roughly sevenfold year-on-year to **HK$10.7 billion**.
- The initial batch is focused on **tokenised money market funds**; the SFC will review and consider
  widening the product scope.

This is the single most useful development for us: it connects stablecoins → tokenised funds → a retail
investor, which is exactly the chain a family financial plan can actually use.

### Virtual asset trading platforms — 13 licensed

Up from the **11** cited in the 2025 case. Licensees include OSL Digital Securities, HashKey Exchange,
PantherTrade (Futu), YAX (Tiger Brokers), Bullish and Victory Fintech (VDX). The authoritative list is the
[SFC VATP list](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Welcome-to-the-Fintech-Contact-Point/Virtual-assets/Virtual-asset-trading-platforms-operators/Lists-of-virtual-asset-trading-platforms)
— check it on the day rather than quoting this number.

### VA dealing and custodian licensing — still a bill, not a law

The 2025 case described these as "undergoing public consultation." Status now:

- **24 December 2025** — FSTB and SFC published [consultation conclusions](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202512/24/P2025122400265.htm)
  on regulating VA dealing and custodian services. Framework sits under the AMLO, on a "same business,
  same risks, same rules" principle.
- A **draft bill was targeted for introduction to LegCo during 2026**. As far as I can establish it has
  **not** yet been enacted — treat as proposed, not law, and re-check before submission.
- A further SFC consultation on licensing **VA advisory and management** service providers closed
  **23 January 2026**.
- Notable design point: **no transitional grace period** on commencement, though expedited processing is
  offered to existing licensed VATPs, licensed corporations and registered institutions.

**Care here.** Recommending an unlicensed custodian arrangement on the assumption this regime is live
would be a factual error a practitioner judge would catch immediately.

### Virtual asset ETFs and staking

- Spot VA ETFs have traded in Hong Kong since **2024** and are retail-accessible.
- The SFC permitted **staking** by licensed platforms and authorised funds (**April 2025** — verify the
  date), only through SFC-licensed VATPs or authorised financial institutions, under asset-control and
  risk-disclosure conditions, subject to a cap set by the management company.
- **February 2026** — SFC announced a licensing regime for OTC trading and a review of VA derivatives and
  margin trading, under its **ASPIRe** roadmap. VA derivatives are aimed at **professional investors**,
  not retail. Do not put a retail family into VA derivatives.

### Tokenised and digital green bonds

- HKSAR has issued **three** digital green bond offerings: the world's first tokenised government green
  bond (2023), the first multi-currency digital bond (2024), and a third offering in **November 2025**
  — the largest at the time, and the first to integrate tokenised central bank money (e-CNY and e-HKD).
- Cumulative tokenised government green and infrastructure issuance now exceeds **US$2 billion**.
- **5 June 2026** — HKMA established a [Tokenised Bond Expert Group](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260605-3/)
  of industry representatives to drive adoption and scalability, exploring policy measures, market
  practices and innovations.
- HKMA maintains **EvergreenHub**, a digital bond knowledge repository.
- **Project Ensemble** — HKMA's tokenisation initiative covering tokenised deposits and wholesale CBDC
  settlement rails, with cross-border and multi-asset ambitions.

**Caveat for a family plan:** government digital green bonds have been institutional issuances. Do not
imply the Lees can buy one at retail without checking distribution.

### ESG — disclosure is tightening, which is the greenwashing answer

- **SFC ESG funds circular** (June 2021, superseding 2019): from **1 January 2022**, ESG funds must conduct
  and disclose **periodic assessment** of how ESG factors are incorporated into strategy, risk management
  and investor communication. A fund's **name must not be misleading**, and ESG references in name or
  marketing must reflect actual features **without exaggeration**.
- **HKEX New Climate Requirements**, built on **IFRS S2** under a "climate-first" approach: comply-or-explain
  for all Main Board issuers from **1 January 2025**, and **mandatory** for Hang Seng Composite LargeCap
  constituents from **1 January 2026**.
- Hong Kong has published **HKFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards**. The government's roadmap requires
  large publicly accountable entities to fully adopt ISSB Standards **no later than 2028**; HKEX will
  consult the market in **2027**.

**Use this against greenwashing.** The scoring move is not "we will buy ESG ETFs." It is: *we will hold
SFC-authorised ESG funds whose periodic assessment disclosures we can inspect, we define the 20% target
against that disclosed methodology, and we re-verify annually because a fund can change its mandate.*

### The three poster headlines, mapped

- **Green finance** → SFC-authorised ESG funds and ETFs, green and sustainability-linked bonds, HKSAR
  digital green bonds, ESG-linked insurance and annuities. Defensible because the disclosure regime is
  real and tightening.
- **Tokenised securities** → tokenised money market funds and the April 2026 secondary-trading framework.
  This is the strongest recommendation available to us: genuinely novel, genuinely 2026, *and* inside the
  SFC-authorised perimeter, so it survives a suitability challenge.
- **Web3** → VA spot ETFs and staking within a capped sleeve; regulated stablecoins for settlement. DeFi
  yield sits outside the perimeter — see below.

### Suitability and ethics

The poster's objectives added *"ethical frameworks"*, which was absent from the 2025 case. Expect to be
asked, implicitly, whether a recommendation is **appropriate**, not merely profitable.

The 2025 case explicitly invited *"decentralized finance (DeFi) products for yield generation."* Our
position should be deliberate rather than reflexive in either direction. DeFi yield sits outside Hong
Kong's regulated perimeter: no licensed intermediary, no client-asset protection, no recourse. For a
household whose retirement floor depends on this capital, recommending it without that being stated is
the failure mode — and recommending nothing at all forfeits the engagement with the theme.

The defensible construction: reach the *same* economic exposure through regulated wrappers wherever one
exists (spot ETF rather than direct holding; SFC-authorised tokenised fund rather than an offshore
protocol), cap the unregulated portion at a size whose total loss is survivable, state that size in terms
of consequence, and attach a de-risking trigger. That scores §4.4's "defend" and §4.5's "safeguards"
simultaneously, and it is what a practitioner judge would actually do.
